# References context

`references/` is for source artifacts, snapshots, extracts and external material retained for traceability.

References are not canonical AQOON truth. They may be outdated, partial or secondary.

Rules:

- prefer official sources for current claims
- label publisher/date/source clearly
- do not commit copyrighted full texts when a citation or short extract is enough
- do not commit private correspondence, family data or confidential material
- promote durable verified facts to `knowledge/`, not by copying an entire reference wholesale
