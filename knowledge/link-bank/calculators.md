# AQOON official calculators and self-service tools

Canonical catalogue of official calculators, estimators, forms and service-finders that may help users understand options before contacting an authority.

## Record format

```yaml
id:
name:
owner:
category:
url:
what_it_calculates_or_checks:
inputs_user_needs: []
result_is: estimate | guidance | official-decision | other
limitations: []
checked_at:
status: verified | needs_recheck | retired
related_services: []
```

## Safety rule

A calculator result is not automatically a formal decision. AQOON must describe the tool exactly as the authority describes it and explain important limitations when present.

## Audit queue

During the Finland-wide research pass, test whether practical access is possible and record the user journey for:

- Kela calculators and benefit estimators
- tax calculators/tools when relevant
- municipal daycare fee calculators where available
- service locators and eligibility questionnaires
- employment-service self-assessment/tools
- education search/filter tools
- housing/consumer official tools when relevant

Record whether the tool requires strong authentication, JavaScript, cookies, a Finnish personal identity code, or other information that may make assisted use difficult.
