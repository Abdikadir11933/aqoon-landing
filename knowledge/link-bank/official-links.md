# AQOON official link bank

Canonical catalogue for official public-service links used by AQOON. This file is intentionally source-oriented. Human-facing Somali copy belongs on `/so` pages.

## Record format

For each link record:

```yaml
id:
label_fi:
label_so:
owner:
category:
jurisdiction:
url:
purpose:
checked_at:
status: verified | needs_recheck | retired
related_pages: []
```

## Coverage queue

The comprehensive Finland audit should populate at least these authority families:

- Suomi.fi
- Kela + OmaKela + Kela calculators
- Työmarkkinatori / municipal employment services
- Migri
- vero.fi / MyTax where relevant
- Studyinfo / Opintopolku
- Finnish National Agency for Education
- YKI official information and registration
- municipalities: Helsinki, Espoo, Vantaa, Tampere first, then broader Finland coverage
- municipal early-childhood education and school services
- wellbeing services counties when relevant
- DVV
- legal aid / authority guidance where relevant
- consumer guidance / KKV for contracts and bills
- official housing and benefit routes where relevant

Do not populate from search snippets. Each record must be opened and checked.
