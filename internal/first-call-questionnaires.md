# AQOON — First-call questionnaires

**Historical reference, not canonical.** This free-text design predates and was superseded by the field-ID-based interview implemented in `tracker/interview-match.js`, `tracker/interview-form-enhancements.js` and `tracker/universal-proof-questions.js`, whose design rationale is `workspaces/evidence-and-research/references/interview-instrument-design-2026-08-27.md`. Flagged as an orphaned duplicate source of truth during the 28 Aug 2026 full-repository audit: none of the code reads this file, and its "5–10 minutes" target and question wording no longer match production. Kept for historical context only; do not treat it as the current interview design.

Purpose: collect only the facts needed to understand the person's situation and match them to the right official service, programme, school, daycare route or support. Do not promise eligibility on the call. The first call should usually take about 5–10 minutes.

## Universal opening

Use a simple opening:

- Confirm name and city.
- Say: “Waxaan rabaa inaan si kooban u fahmo xaaladdaada si aan kuu helo meesha ama barnaamijka saxda ah. Waxaan ku weydiinayaa dhowr su’aalood, kadib waan hubin doonaa oo waan kula soo xiriiri doonaa tallaabada xigta.”

Always capture:

- Name
- Phone
- City / municipality
- Preferred language
- What they want to achieve, in their own words
- How urgent it is / target date
- Best time to call back

# 1. CHILD / FAMILY HELP

Start with:

**“Maxaad ilmahaaga hadda ugu baahan tahay caawimaad?”**

Choose route:
- Daycare / päiväkoti
- Esiopetus
- School support
- Hobby / activity
- Something else

## A. Daycare / päiväkoti

Ask:

1. How old is the child?
2. Which city do you live in?
3. Does the child already have a daycare place?
4. When do you need the place to start?
5. Why do you need daycare now?
   - work starting
   - studies starting
   - Finnish/integration course
   - family need
   - other
6. Have you already submitted an application?
7. If yes, when and through which system?
8. Municipal daycare only, or are private/service-voucher options also okay?
9. Is there a preferred area/neighbourhood?
10. Do you need help filling the application, understanding the decision, or finding the right daycare?

Useful routing facts:
- city
- child age
- required start date
- whether need is sudden due to work/study
- application already submitted or not
- municipal/private flexibility

Likely next routes:
- city early childhood education application
- city-specific daycare route
- service voucher/private route if suitable

## B. Esiopetus

Ask:

1. Child’s age and birth year?
2. City and neighbourhood?
3. Is the child currently in daycare?
4. Have you received a preschool / esiopetus message or placement?
5. Has the child already been registered?
6. Do you need help understanding the placement, registering, changing location, or arranging care before/after preschool?
7. Does the child need Finnish-language or other learning support?

## C. School support

Ask:

1. Child’s age and school grade?
2. Which school/city?
3. What is the main concern?
   - Finnish/S2
   - reading/writing
   - maths
   - behaviour
   - concentration
   - bullying
   - special support
   - communication with school/Wilma
4. Has the school already discussed support with you?
5. Have you received any Wilma message, meeting invite or decision?
6. What support is already being given, if any?
7. What would you like to happen next?
8. Do you want help understanding a message/decision, preparing for a meeting, or finding the correct school contact?

Do not diagnose the child or promise special education. Route to school/municipality support process.

## D. Hobby / activity

Ask:

1. Child’s age and school grade?
2. City and neighbourhood?
3. What does the child like?
   - football/sports
   - swimming
   - dance
   - art/music
   - coding/gaming
   - something else
4. Does it need to be free, or is a small fee okay?
5. How far can the family travel?
6. Which days/times are possible?
7. Any language/accessibility needs?
8. Has the child already tried a hobby?

Likely routes:
- Harrastamisen Suomen malli Helsinki
- Harrastusten Vantaa
- city/local free activity programmes

# 2. EDUCATION / SCHOOL FOR THE ADULT

Start with:

**“Maxaad rabtaa inaad gaarto: Finnish barasho, shahaado, ammatti, ama inaad dib ugu noqoto dugsi?”**

Ask every adult education lead:

1. Age?
2. City?
3. How long have you lived in Finland?
4. Are you currently registered as a jobseeker?
5. Are you working now? Full-time / part-time / not working?
6. Are you caring for children at home?
7. What is your current Finnish level approximately?
   - almost none
   - basic
   - can manage daily life
   - intermediate
   - not sure
8. What education have you completed before?
9. Do you have certificates/diplomas from your previous country?
10. What is your main goal for the next 6–12 months?

## A. Finnish course

Ask:

1. Have you studied Finnish before? Where/how long?
2. Can you read and write in your own language?
3. Do you need beginner Finnish or a higher-level course?
4. Daytime or evening?
5. Online okay or in-person only?
6. Do you need childcare to attend?
7. Are you a jobseeker or in an integration plan?
8. Do you need Finnish mainly for work, school, YKI/citizenship, or everyday life?

Possible routing:
- city integration services
- employment-service integration/Finnish training
- VAMU if Vantaa parent
- Espoo home-parent Finnish courses
- Finnishcourses.fi / adult education providers

## B. YKI

Ask:

1. Why do you need YKI?
   - citizenship
   - work
   - school
   - personal goal
2. Have you taken YKI before?
3. Which level do you think you need?
4. Current Finnish level?
5. Do you want help finding test dates, registering, or preparing?
6. Is there a deadline for citizenship/job/studies?

Do not tell them a specific YKI result guarantees citizenship. Verify Migri/official requirements where relevant.

## C. “I want a school / qualification / profession”

Ask:

1. What is the highest school/qualification you completed?
2. Do you have documents/certificates?
3. What did you study or work as before?
4. What job would you like in Finland?
5. Are you open to vocational education / ammattikoulu?
6. Full-time study possible?
7. Need work alongside studies?
8. Current Finnish level?
9. Are you a registered jobseeker?
10. Do you need a fast employment route or a longer qualification route?

Possible routing:
- Omnia OSKE / KOSKE in Espoo
- Osaamiskeskus Vantaa
- Stadin AO immigrant training
- vocational education / Studyinfo
- adult basic education if foundational qualification is missing

## D. Highly educated abroad

Ask:

1. What degree do you have?
2. Which country/institution?
3. Do you have the diploma and transcript?
4. What was your profession/work experience?
5. Is the profession regulated in Finland?
6. Have you applied for recognition/equivalence before?
7. Finnish/Swedish level?
8. Are you unemployed and registered as a jobseeker?
9. Do you want your own field, or are you open to another route?

Possible route:
- KOSKE / Talent Hub-type services
- regulated-profession recognition authority where applicable

# 3. JOB / WORK FOR THE ADULT

Start with:

**“Hadda maxaad ugu baahan tahay shaqo ahaan: shaqo helid, CV/codsi, tababar, programme, ama xirfad cusub?”**

Ask every work lead:

1. City?
2. Age?
3. Are you registered as a jobseeker now?
4. How long have you been unemployed, if unemployed?
5. Are you receiving unemployment-related support?
6. What jobs have you done before, in Finland or elsewhere?
7. What type of job do you want now?
8. Finnish level?
9. Other languages?
10. Driving licence?
11. Any work cards/certificates?
   - hygieniapassi
   - työturvallisuuskortti
   - anniskelupassi
   - first aid
   - other
12. Can you work evenings/weekends?
13. Full-time or part-time?
14. How far can you travel?
15. When can you start?

## A. Needs a job now

Ask additionally:

1. Do you have a CV?
2. When did you last update it?
3. Have you applied for jobs in the last month?
4. Which jobs/sites have you tried?
5. Are you getting interviews?
6. What is the biggest obstacle right now?
   - language
   - CV/application
   - no Finnish experience
   - no qualification
   - childcare
   - transport
   - not sure where to apply

Possible routing:
- direct vacancies
- CV/application help
- employment services
- Osaamiskeskus / OSKE
- palkkatuki discussion if appropriate

## B. Work programme / training

Ask:

1. Are you willing to train for a new field?
2. Which fields interest you?
3. How long can you study/train before needing income?
4. Are you an employment-services customer?
5. Would S2/language-supported training help?
6. Any physical limitations relevant to the job? Only ask if necessary for the chosen work route, not as a general health questionnaire.
7. Can you attend daytime training?

Possible routing:
- Stadin AO labour-market training
- Kielituettu ryhmävalmennus
- Osaamiskeskus Vantaa
- OSKE Espoo
- other current `/caawi/ajankohtaiset` programmes

## C. Palkkatuki

Ask:

1. Are you registered as an unemployed jobseeker?
2. Has your employment expert ever mentioned palkkatuki?
3. Do you already have an employer interested in hiring you?
4. Has the employer asked whether wage subsidy is possible?
5. How long have you been unemployed?

Next step: tell them AQOON will help them ask their own employment service whether palkkatuki may be available. Never promise it.

## D. Work cards / certificates

Ask:

1. Which job are you trying to get?
2. Has an employer told you which card is required?
3. Which cards do you already have?
4. Which city?
5. Finnish/English reading level for the test?
6. How quickly do you need it?
7. Need help finding a course/test, studying, or registering?

## E. Entrepreneurship

Ask:

1. What do you want to sell/do?
2. Is the business already operating or only an idea?
3. Do you have a Y-tunnus?
4. Have you invoiced customers before?
5. Do you expect this to be full-time or side income?
6. Are you currently unemployed/jobseeker?
7. Do you need help with start-up advice, registration, pricing, invoicing, or funding?

Route to official enterprise advisory / employment services before making benefit or starttiraha assumptions.

# 4. PARENT / INTEGRATION HELP

Use this when the person says something broad like “I am home with children”, “I want to integrate”, “I need Finnish”, “I don’t know what I can do”.

Ask:

1. City?
2. How long in Finland?
3. Residence permit valid?
4. Are you registered as a jobseeker?
5. Are you at home caring for children?
6. Ages of children?
7. Can you attend activities if childcare is available?
8. Finnish level?
9. Have you had an integration plan before?
10. Main goal right now:
   - learn Finnish
   - meet people / understand Finland
   - school
   - work
   - childcare/daycare
   - all of these

Strong routes:
- VAMU in Vantaa
- Helsinki non-jobseeker integration services
- Espoo home-parent Finnish courses
- city integration/employment services

---

# 5. YOUTH 15–29

Ask:

1. Age?
2. City?
3. Are you in school now?
4. Are you working now?
5. What is the biggest problem today?
   - no school place
   - no job
   - unsure what to study
   - housing
   - money
   - wellbeing
   - documents / immigration question
6. Do you have a CV?
7. Completed basic school / secondary education?
8. Do you want immediate work or first need help choosing a direction?

Possible route:
- Ohjaamo
- Mun suunnat, mun polut
- vocational / youth employment programmes
- summer-job support when seasonal

---

# 6. END EVERY FIRST CALL THE SAME WAY

Summarise back:

- “Waxaad ii sheegtay inaad rabto ___.”
- “Waxyaabaha muhiimka ah waa: magaalada ___, xaaladdaadu ___, waxaadna rabtaa inaad bilowdo ___.”
- “Waxaan hadda hubinayaa fursadaha iyo shuruudaha rasmiga ah. Kadib waxaan kuu soo dirayaa ama kuu sheegayaa 1–3 tallaabo oo sax ah.”

Record after the call:

- Main goal
- Relevant facts / eligibility clues
- Documents they already have
- Documents/messages they need help understanding
- Likely programme/service candidates
- What must be verified
- Next action for AQOON
- Follow-up date

## Important rule

Do not overwhelm the caller with five programmes. The follow-up should normally give:

1. **Best next route**
2. **One backup route if useful**
3. **Exact next action** — link, application, phone number, meeting, document, or registration

That is the AQOON painkiller: not more information, but the correct next move.
