### Helsinki — Aikuisten TUVA is now open

- Organiser: Stadin AO / City of Helsinki.
- **Application is now open 10.9.–4.11.2026.** Studies start 25.11.2026 and selection results are sent 16.11.2026.
- Adult places are specifically for applicants over 18 who want to develop Finnish through S2 teaching.
- Published general TUVA criteria: applicant is compulsory-school-age or an adult needing readiness, guidance and support before vocational/upper-secondary study; has not completed upper-secondary or higher education in Finland; and has a comprehensive-school leaving certificate or certificate of equivalent previous education.
- Application route: the `Täytä hakemus` link on Stadin AO's TUVA page (Stadin Wilma).
- Full-time study, maximum 38 weeks. Adult locations: Roihupelto and Teollisuuskatu.
- The checked application page does not state a separate Helsinki-residency condition or tuition fee; verify costs/support individually before promising.
- Public-page action: `/caawi/ajankohtaiset` is stale because it still says the previous round closes “today 9.9.” Replace that text with the active 10.9.–4.11 round and 25.11 start.
- Official: https://stadinao.hel.fi/hae-opiskelemaan/tuva/

- Organiser: Stadin AO / Helsinki Employment Services.
- Fits: Helsinki residents aged 18–29 who need support choosing a field, applying to studies, developing study/work readiness, or identifying skills and strengths.
- Finnish requirement: at least A2.1.
- Next group: **15.9.–7.10.2026**, Tue–Wed 9–12 at Teollisuuskatu. Later groups: 13.10.–4.11 and 10.11.–2.12.

### Helsinki — Oppisopimusvalmennus (new registry gap)

- Organiser: Stadin AO / Helsinki Employment Services.
- Fits: Helsinki jobseekers who need support finding an apprenticeship place and whose language/study readiness is sufficient for the qualification.
- Duration: about one month; group and individual guidance, and when needed a guided work trial.
- Helps with: apprenticeship rules, study route, qualification, job search, and finding a work-trial/apprenticeship place.
- Schedule: group meets 2–3 times/week for about three hours at Teollisuuskatu.
- **Availability: non-stop / can be referred at any time of year.**
- Route: the referring employment-services expert enrols the client in Wilma.
- Checked page does not state a participant fee; do not promise free participation without separate verification.
- Public-page recommendation: strong candidate to add under work/education because it is a year-round concrete apprenticeship pathway.
- Official: https://stadinao.hel.fi/hae-opiskelemaan/palvelut-tyollistymiseen-ja-opintoihin/ohjausta-omalle-uralle/
