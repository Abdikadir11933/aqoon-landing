# AQOON — Open programmes & active routes

Last compiled: 26.8.2026

Purpose: quick internal reference for active programmes, hankkeet and services that may fit AQOON leads. Always re-check the official source before promising availability or eligibility.

## Priority routes

### Vantaa

- **Vanhemmat mukaan (VAMU)** — Vantaan kaupunki
  - Fits: immigrant parents, including stay-at-home parents; Finnish not required; child can come along.
  - Helps with: Finnish, integration, understanding school/daycare, path toward studies/work.
  - Status: active/open.
  - Official: https://www.vantaa.fi/fi/hankkeet/hanke/vanhemmat-mukaan

- **Osaamiskeskus Vantaa** — Vantaan kaupunki
  - Fits: immigrant jobseekers in Vantaa.
  - Helps with: skills recognition, education, Finnish, digital skills, career guidance, employment path.
  - Status: active; usually through employment services/referral.
  - Official: https://www.vantaa.fi/fi/tyonhaku-ja-tyollistaminen/olen-muuttanut-suomeen-ja-haluan-toihin

- **Vantaan työllisyys- ja kotoutumispalvelut** — Vantaan kaupunki
  - Fits: jobseekers and immigrants in Vantaa.
  - Helps with: work, training, integration plan, Finnish course, correct service path.
  - Status: active.
  - Official: https://www.vantaa.fi/fi/tyonhaku-ja-tyollistaminen/olen-muuttanut-suomeen-ja-haluan-toihin

- **Vantaan varhaiskasvatus** — Vantaan kaupunki
  - Fits: Vantaa families needing daycare / early childhood education.
  - Helps with: municipal daycare application and correct route.
  - Status: active.
  - Official: https://www.vantaa.fi/en/education-and-learning/early-childhood-education/applying-early-childhood-education

- **Harrastusten Vantaa 2026–2027** — Vantaan kaupunki
  - Fits: Vantaa comprehensive-school pupils.
  - Helps with: free supervised hobbies.
  - Status: registration opened 24.8.2026.
  - Official: https://www.vantaa.fi/fi/ajankohtaista/uutinen/ilmoittautuminen-koululaisten-maksuttomiin-harrastuksiin-alkaa-248

- **Vantaan nuorten kesätyöseteli** — Vantaan kaupunki
  - Fits: eligible young people in Vantaa.
  - Helps with: first summer job; employer gets support.
  - Deadline: 31.8.2026.
  - Official: https://www.vantaa.fi/fi/palveluhakemisto/palvelu/nuorten-kesatyoseteli

### Helsinki

- **Ohjaamo Helsinki** — Helsingin kaupunki
  - Fits: Helsinki residents aged 15–29.
  - Helps with: work, school, CV, housing, money, wellbeing, immigration questions.
  - Status: active / walk-in.
  - Official: https://nuorten.hel.fi/opiskelu-ja-tyo/ohjaamo/

- **Integration services for those who are not jobseekers** — Helsingin kaupunki
  - Fits: immigrants in Helsinki who are not registered jobseekers, including parents caring for children at home.
  - Helps with: Finnish/Swedish, integration plan, orientation, groups and other integration support.
  - Status: active.
  - Official: https://www.hel.fi/en/business-and-work/jobseekers/if-you-become-unemployed/when-you-have-moved-to-finland/integration-services-for-those-who-are-not-jobseekers

- **Stadin AO työvoimakoulutukset maahanmuuttajille**
  - Fits: immigrant unemployed jobseekers or people at risk of unemployment.
  - Helps with: profession + S2 support + route to work.
  - Current known deadline: 7.9.2026; individual programmes can close earlier.
  - Official: https://stadinao.hel.fi/hae-opiskelemaan/palvelut-tyollistymiseen-ja-opintoihin/koulutukset-ja-palvelut-maahanmuuttajille/tyovoimakoulutukset-maahanmuuttajille/

- **Kielituettu ryhmävalmennus** — Stadin AO / Helsinki employment services
  - Fits: unemployed Helsinki residents whose work/study direction is unclear.
  - Helps with: Finnish, digital skills, work-life skills, personal next-step plan.
  - Status: active, several autumn 2026 groups.
  - Official: https://stadinao.hel.fi/hae-opiskelemaan/palvelut-tyollistymiseen-ja-opintoihin/koulutukset-ja-palvelut-maahanmuuttajille/kielituettu-ryhmavalmennus/

- **Kohti työelämää — kitchen & cleaning short training** — Stadin AO / Helsinki employment services
  - Fits: Helsinki employment-service clients.
  - Helps with: short training and possible route to school/daycare kitchen or cleaning work.
  - Recruitment event: 21.9.2026.
  - Official: https://stadinao.hel.fi/hae-opiskelemaan/palvelut-tyollistymiseen-ja-opintoihin/lupa-ja-korttikoulutukset/

- **Maahan muuttaneiden opettajien polku** — Stadin AO
  - Fits: immigrant teachers, early-childhood teachers and pedagogues.
  - Helps with: route into Finnish education/early-childhood work.
  - Status: ongoing.
  - Official: https://stadinao.hel.fi/hae-opiskelemaan/palvelut-tyollistymiseen-ja-opintoihin/koulutukset-ja-palvelut-maahanmuuttajille/maahan-muuttaneiden-opettajien-polku/

- **Harrastamisen Suomen malli / Helsinki** — Helsingin kaupunki
  - Fits: Helsinki pupils in grades 3–9.
  - Helps with: free hobbies after school.
  - Status: active / registration opened 24.8.2026.
  - Official: https://nuorten.hel.fi/en/things-to-do-and-places/free-of-charge-leisure-activities-for-pupils-in-grades-3-9/

- **Mun suunnat, mun polut** — Ohjaamo Helsinki
  - Fits: ages 15–29 who do not know their study/work direction.
  - Starts: 14.9.2026.
  - Official: https://nuorten.hel.fi/tapahtuma/?event_id=helsinki%3Aagpr2irxsi

### Espoo

- **Omnian osaamiskeskus maahanmuuttajille (OSKE)** — Omnia / Espoo employment services
  - Fits: adult immigrants in Espoo.
  - Helps with: skills recognition, vocational education, job search, study guidance, apprenticeship routes.
  - Status: active/open.
  - Official: https://www.espoo.fi/fi/tyoelama/tyollisyyspalvelut/keinoja-paasta-tyoelamaan

- **KOSKE** — Espoo employment services
  - Fits: unemployed immigrants with a higher-education degree from abroad.
  - Helps with: own-field career path, job search, career guidance.
  - Status: active/open.
  - Official: https://www.espoo.fi/fi/tyoelama/tyollisyyspalvelut/keinoja-paasta-tyoelamaan

- **Kotivanhempien suomen kielen kurssit** — Espoo / Omnia / Koto-Espoo
  - Fits: immigrant stay-at-home parents.
  - Helps with: Finnish learning where childcare would otherwise block participation.
  - Status: active service model in 2026.
  - Official: https://www.espoo.fi/fi/uutiset/2026/01/kotivanhemmille-suunnatut-suomen-kielen-kurssit-vakiintuvat-osaksi-espoon-kotoutumispalveluita

### Tampere

- **Arjen avaimet 2026–2027** — Tampereen seudun työväenopisto / Tampereen kaupunki
  - Fits: immigrant women outside work, insufficient Finnish, including some spouses and people beyond the integration period.
  - Helps with: Finnish, job search, society, digital skills, personal finances.
  - Selection test: 3.9.2026.
  - Official: https://www.tampere.fi/kaupunki-ja-paatoksenteko/organisaatio/arjen-avaimet-maahanmuuttajanaisten-tyollistymista-edistava-koulutus

### Finland-wide / case-by-case

- **Palkkatuki**
  - Fits: some unemployed jobseekers where employment services assess that wage subsidy may help employment.
  - Important: paid to the employer, not the worker; never promise eligibility.
  - Official starting point: https://tyomarkkinatori.fi/henkiloasiakkaat/tyottomyysturva/tyollistymista-edistavat-palvelut/palkkatuki

## Partnership / outreach priorities

Current strongest AQOON demand signals:

1. Vantaan varhaiskasvatus — live daycare demand.
2. Vantaan työllisyys- ja kotoutumispalvelut / Osaamiskeskus — live work + Finnish/education demand.
3. Harrastamisen Suomen malli Helsinki — live child-hobby demand.
4. VAMU — strong match to Somali parent segment.
5. Stadin AO immigrant employment training — strong job/training match.
6. Omnia OSKE / KOSKE — strong Espoo work/education routes.

## QA rule

Before giving any user a programme as “open now”, re-check:
- programme is still active,
- correct city/residence requirement,
- age/status requirements,
- whether registration is direct or needs employment-service referral,
- deadline,
- whether childcare/language support really applies,
- official application/contact link.
