AQOON · Waxbarasho Finland

Ammatillinen koulutus ma kuu noqon kartaa waddo?

Vocational education-ka Finland waxaa jira qualifications iyo habab codsi oo kala duwan. AQOON ma sheegi karo dugsiga ama qualification-ka kuu fiican anigoon xaaladdaada fahmin. Waxaan kaa caawin karnaa inaad qeexdo waxa aad rabto inaad barato, hesho programmes-ka rasmiga ah ee Studyinfo, fahanto application period-ka iyo cidda school-ka lagala xiriirayo.

Waxaan kaa caawin karnaa

  • Raadinta programme ama qualification.
  • Fahamka Finnish-ka job/education vocabulary.
  • Helidda continuous application haddii ay jirto programme-ka aad eegayso.
  • Diyaarinta su'aalaha aad school-ka waydiinayso.

Shuruudaha gelitaanka, luqadda iyo application dates waxay ku xirnaanayaan programme-ka. Had iyo jeer waxaan ku laabaneynaa bogga rasmiga ah ee dugsiga ama Studyinfo.
