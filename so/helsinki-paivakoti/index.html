AQOON · Helsinki

Helsinki päiväkoti codsi ma u baahan tahay?

Helsinki waxay sheegaysaa in municipal early childhood education caadi ahaan la codsado ugu yaraan afar bilood ka hor inta ilmahaagu u baahan yahay boos. Primary online service-ku waa Edlevo, waxaana jira paper route haddii online-ku suurtagal ahayn. AQOON ma go'aaminayo urgent status ama placement-ka, laakiin waxaan kaa caawin karnaa inaad fahanto official instructions oo aad gaarto service guidance-ka saxda ah.

Helsinki sidoo kale waxay sheegaysaa in “sudden and unforeseen” cases ay leeyihiin process gaar ah iyo proof. Haddii xaaladdaadu sidaas tahay, bogga rasmiga ah ayaan kula hubin doonaa halkii aan ka qiyaasi lahayn.

Waxaan kaa caawin karnaa

  • Edlevo iyo application-ka.
  • Paper application haddii loo baahdo.
  • Application preferences iyo start date.
  • Esiopetus iyo early childhood service guidance.
