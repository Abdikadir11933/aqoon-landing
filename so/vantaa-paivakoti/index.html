AQOON · Vantaa

Vantaa päiväkoti codsi ma u baahan tahay?

Vantaa waxay sheegaysaa in early childhood education application caadi ahaan la diro ugu dambayn afar bilood ka hor marka ilmahaagu u baahan yahay boos. Codsiga waxaa lagu sameeyaa VaSa; municipal, private iyo service voucher options ayaa jira. AQOON ma go'aamiyo booska, laakiin waxaan kaa caawin karnaa inaad fahanto options-ka, buuxiso jidka saxda ah oo aad la xiriirto service guidance haddii loo baahdo.

Haddii aad ogtahay inaad shaqo, waxbarasho ama päiväkoti u baahan doonto bilaha soo socda, ha sugin ilaa todobaadka ugu dambeeya.

Waxaan kaa caawin karnaa

  • VaSa service iyo login.
  • Municipal vs private/service voucher.
  • Application preferences iyo start date.
  • Esiopetus haddii ilmuhu gaarayo da'daas.
