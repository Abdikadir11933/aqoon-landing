Ilmahaaga dugsiga taageero ma u baahan yahay?

Haddii aad heshay Wilma message, teacher ayaa kula soo xiriiray, ama aad ka walwalsan tahay Finnish-ka, waxbarashada ama taageerada ilmaha, AQOON ma qiimeeyo baahida waxbarasho mana beddelo school professionals. Waxaan kaa caawin karnaa inaad fahanto fariinta, diyaarsato su'aalaha, hesho teacher/school contact-ka saxda ah iyo bogga degmada ee taageerada.
