AQOON · Shaqo Finland

Shaqo la'aan tahay oo ma hubtid sida työnhakija loo noqdo?

Employment services-ka Finland waxay isticmaalaan Job Market Finland (Työmarkkinatori) adeegyo badan. AQOON ma go'aamiso xaaladdaada shaqo-doon ama benefit-kaaga. Waxaan kaa caawin karnaa inaad hesho bogga saxda ah, fahanto waxa “register as a jobseeker” ka dhigan yahay, iyo cidda degmadaada ka mas'uulka ah employment services.

Haddii Kela ama employment services ay kuu soo diraan deadline, ha sugin. Waxaan kaa caawin karnaa inaad fahanto waxa qoraalka rasmiga ahi leeyahay, laakiin hay'adda rasmiga ah ayaa go'aanka sameysa.

Waxaan kaa caawin karnaa

  • Helidda Job Market Finland.
  • Fahamka työnhakija, työllisyyspalvelut iyo shaqo-doon active ah.
  • Helidda degmadaada employment services.
  • Kala saaridda shaqo raadis, tababar ama integration-related services.
