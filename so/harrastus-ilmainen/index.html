Ilmahaaga hobby ama ciyaar bilaash ah ma u raadineysaa?

Magaalooyin badan waxay leeyihiin school-based hobbies, youth activities, clubs ama programmes lacag la'aan ama qiimo jaban ah. Magacyada, da'da iyo registration-ku way kala duwan yihiin. AQOON kuma ballanqaadayo boos, laakiin waxaan kaa caawin karnaa inaad raadiso programme-ka magaaladaada, fahanto da'da/waqtiga, hesho registration link-ka iyo qofka saxda ah ee la waydiiyo.

Waxaan raadin karnaa

  • School hobbies iyo Harrastamisen Suomen malli.
  • Sports, art, music iyo clubs.
  • Youth services iyo activities.
  • Programmes gaar ah oo xilliyada fasaxa ah.

Barnaamijyada way is beddelaan xilli kasta. Waxaan isticmaali doonaa bogga magaalada, school-ka ama organiser-ka rasmiga ah.
