Espoo päiväkoti codsi ma u baahan tahay?

Espoo waxay sheegaysaa in xannaanada carruurta ee degmada la codsado ugu yaraan afar bilood ka hor taariikhda aad rabto inuu ilmuhu bilaabo. eVaka ayaa loo isticmaalaa codsiga, waxaana lagu dooran karaa xannaano degmo ama palveluseteli (voucher-ka adeegga). AQOON ma go'aaminayo meesha la siin doono ama qiimaha, laakiin waxaan kaa caawin karnaa inaad fahanto tallaabooyinka codsiga oo aad gaarto hagidda adeegga saxda ah.

Espoo waxay sidoo kale leedahay free early childhood education arrangement for five-year-olds. Haddii taasi ilmahaaga khusayso, waxaan kaa caawin karnaa inaad bogga rasmiga ah ka hubiso details-ka hadda jira.
