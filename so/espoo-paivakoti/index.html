Espoo päiväkoti codsi ma u baahan tahay?

Espoo waxay sheegaysaa in municipal early childhood education la codsado ugu yaraan afar bilood ka hor taariikhda aad rabto inuu ilmuhu bilaabo. eVaka ayaa loo isticmaalaa codsiga, waxaana lagu dooran karaa municipal iyo service voucher options. AQOON ma go'aaminayo placement-ka ama fee-ga, laakiin waxaan kaa caawin karnaa inaad fahanto process-ka oo aad gaarto service guidance-ka saxda ah.

Espoo waxay sidoo kale leedahay free early childhood education arrangement for five-year-olds. Haddii taasi ilmahaaga khusayso, waxaan kaa caawin karnaa inaad bogga rasmiga ah ka hubiso details-ka hadda jira.
