# `/so` context

Purpose: Somali-language public guidance that helps people in Finland understand the right route, official service, programme or next step.

## Language contract

Write like a clear Somali-speaking person explaining something over WhatsApp, cleaned up for a public website.

- Short sentences.
- Direct second-person address.
- Avoid bureaucratic Somali and literal Finnish/English calques.
- Keep familiar Finnish service/system names when recognition matters: Kela, OmaKela, päiväkoti, esiopetus, Wilma, YKI, Työmarkkinatori, työllisyyspalvelut, palkkatuki, palveluseteli, kotihoidon tuki, asumistuki, toimeentulotuki, hygieniapassi, työturvallisuuskortti.
- Explain the Finnish term immediately in Somali when needed.
- Do not leave whole Finnish explanatory sentences inside Somali copy.
- Prefer `bogga rasmiga ah`, `linkiga rasmiga ah`, `taariikhda ugu dambeysa`, `codsi`, `isdiiwaangelin`, `shuruudaha`, `taageero`, `adeeg` over unnecessary English words.

## Truth contract

The page text is a presentation layer, not the source of truth.

Before publishing time-sensitive or eligibility-related content, verify the relevant canonical record in `knowledge/` against a current official source.

Never present AQOON as a public authority, Kela, municipality, employment service, school or legal adviser. Never guarantee entitlement, acceptance, benefit amount, deadline exceptions or authority outcomes.

## Content model

Prefer this order on topic pages:

1. What this is / why the user might care.
2. Who usually owns the issue (authority/service).
3. What the user should check.
4. What the next action is.
5. Official source/link.
6. AQOON help CTA.

## Link-bank rule

Official links and calculators should be catalogued centrally under `knowledge/link-bank/` even when they are also linked from a page.

## Current opportunities

`/so/ajankohtaiset` is volatile. Every programme/deadline must have a checked date and current official source. Remove or retire expired items rather than leaving them as evergreen truth.

## QA before publish

1. Somali sounds natural and modern.
2. Finnish service names are retained where useful for real-world recognition.
3. No unsupported entitlement/legal claim.
4. Every volatile claim has a current official source.
5. City-specific rules are not generalized to all Finland.
6. Links work and point to the intended authority/provider.
7. Page gives a concrete next step rather than only background information.
