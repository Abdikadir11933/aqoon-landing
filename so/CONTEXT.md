Language:

- keep familiar Finnish system names when recognition matters.

Facts:

- high/live claims must be rechecked before publication.
- programme/deadline pages are not trustworthy merely because old HTML says they are current.

Safety:
AQOON explains and navigates; authorities/providers decide outcomes.
