# Evidence & Research Context

Use official primary sources for law, entitlement and public procedures; provider primary sources for provider-specific details; AQOON primary evidence for AQOON-owned observations. Maintain source IDs, effective dates, volatility and verification state.

Discovery materials are leads, not truth. Failed verification remains visible as `verification_pending`.

## AQOON primary evidence hierarchy

For claims about AQOON's own model, prefer observed behaviour over stated intent:

1. verified outcome: completed application/registration, signed agreement, service start or persistence follow-up;
2. verified action: application/registration started or next step completed;
3. first interview: need and relevant criteria confirmed directly with the family;
4. identifiable contact: valid name/phone voluntarily provided;
5. high-intent engagement: direct message/comment requesting help;
6. community distribution: shares/saves/follows;
7. reach: views, sessions and impressions.

Do not collapse these levels. A view is not a lead. A saved phone is not a completed intake. A completed intake is not an outcome.

## Client / pilot reporting

Client reports may use anonymised aggregate AQOON evidence, but buyer-specific commercial strategy, named family records and private sales notes do not belong in public GitHub.

For programme-specific reporting, preserve the exact promised outcome chain. Example: a hobby pilot should separate awareness, eligible child, registration help, registration, start and persistence; a daycare funnel should separate awareness, application route, application/action and verified daycare start.

Early rates must be labelled provisional when the cohort is small, incomplete or observed for less than a full reporting window.
