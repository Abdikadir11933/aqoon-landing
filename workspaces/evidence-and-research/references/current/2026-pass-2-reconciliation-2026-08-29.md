# Pass 2 reconciliation — study, training and return to work

Checked: 29 August 2026. This record reconciles the earlier AQOON Pass 2 route seed with a stronger submitted research ledger and current official sources.

## Confirmed additions

- Labour-market training can be vocational, entrepreneurial or integration training. It is organised by the employment authority, selected by that authority and is generally free apart from possible card/licence-course fees.
- Kela's current employment-promoting-services page lists labour-market training, self-motivated study, immigrant self-motivated study, job-search coaching, career coaching and work trials. It says Kela pays the general social security benefit during participation in agreed services.
- The same Kela page lists an expense compensation of EUR 9/day, up to five days a week, for specified services, and EUR 18/day under its stated distance/accommodation conditions. The list does not justify a blanket promise for generic self-motivated study.
- Job-search coaching and career coaching are separate authority-assessed, employment-plan routes.
- Wage subsidy is employer-paid, is individually discretionary and high volatility; the national page says it is most often 50% of wage costs and often five or ten months, but local availability must be checked at the time of a real referral.

## Not adopted as stable AQOON logic

- Metro path durations, invitations, Testipiste workflow and particular provider/course availability: verify against the current city/area/provider page at referral time.
- Any pending wage-subsidy reform: not current guidance until enacted and verified.
- Benefit, expense-compensation or waiting-period outcomes for a named person: Kela/fund and the employment authority decide.

## Human-review threshold

The national route map is safe for navigation. City-specific recommendations and any wage-subsidy recommendation require current human review before being presented to a family or employer.
