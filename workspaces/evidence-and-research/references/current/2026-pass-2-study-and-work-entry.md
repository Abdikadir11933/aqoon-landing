# Pass 2 — study while unemployed and routes back to work

Checked: 29 August 2026. Scope: national first-contact routes for jobseekers considering study, labour-market training, work trials, wage subsidy or apprenticeship. This pass excludes local course availability, individual benefit decisions, rehabilitation and broad adult-education admissions.

## Verified

- Labour-market training is an employment-authority-organised route and can include integration training.
- Self-motivated study while unemployed has separate short-term, part-time and full-time paths; full-time support requires an authority assessment and should normally be agreed before study starts.
- Immigrant customer's self-motivated study is a distinct integration-plan route, not a blanket right to study with unemployment support.
- A work trial is not an employment relationship; it is an authority-agreed plan route, with a national maximum duration of six months.
- Wage subsidy is paid to the employer, can be relevant to apprenticeship, and must be locally checked before any claim about availability.

## Mandatory operator boundary

Before a jobseeker starts or changes study, AQOON must route them to their employment-services expert and avoid saying that unemployment security will continue. Kela/fund payment follows its own application and decision process.
