# Pass 9 — live national programmes and hankkeet

Date checked: 2026-08-29

## Scope

Population: families and adults in Finland who may benefit from a live public programme or grant round, plus organisations navigating STEA/STM/EU project funding.

Geography: national Finland only. City-specific programmes (Helsinki, Espoo, Vantaa, Tampere and others) are explicitly out of scope for this pass.

Included: youth employment voucher (TEM), Suomi liikkeelle national movement programme (OKM), STEA's 2026 grant proposal and decision context (STM/STEA), youth drug-harm-prevention project funding (STEA/STM/THL), and Finland's EU ESR+/EAKR structural-funds programme 2021–2027 (STM/ELY centres).

Excluded: municipal/city-specific programmes; EU Horizon, Erasmus+, Business Finland, Sitra, church/parish and private-foundation funding (partner inventory remains open); exact application deadlines and per-project capacity beyond what each official page states as of the checked date.

## What is now source-mapped (not yet a verified standing route)

1. A youth employment recruitment voucher (nuorten työllistymisseteli) exists for employers hiring unemployed 18–29-year-olds, under a decree effective 22.12.2025.
2. The Suomi liikkeelle national movement programme is funded at roughly EUR 20 million/year (~EUR 80 million across the government term), with EUR 17 million allocated for 2026 movement projects including early-childhood education and organisation-led trials.
3. STM's 2026 STEA grant proposal is approximately EUR 274 million; new-project funding within it is limited to EU-project co-financing (EUR 1.52 million) and youth drug-harm-prevention projects (EUR 1.0 million) — most of the total sustains already-funded organisation activity, not new applicants.
4. STEA/STM/THL are funding three youth drug-harm-prevention projects (Helsinki, Oulu, one national project) for 2026–2028 with EUR 1.0 million.
5. Finland's EU ESR+/EAKR structural-funds programme 2021–2027 is coordinated through ELY centres and the EURA2021 system; STM participates via employment, social-innovation and poverty-reduction priority lines.

This is deliberately **not** called "verified coverage" for the domain: per the coverage register, "Live programmes, hankkeet, grants and partner offerings" stays a **Live discovery only** domain even after this pass, because open-call status, capacity and deadlines change continuously and no universal current-programme claim can be made from a point-in-time source read.
