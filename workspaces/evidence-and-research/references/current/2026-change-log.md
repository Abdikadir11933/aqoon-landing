# 2026 High-impact Change Log

Last verified: 2026-09-06.

- 1.1.2026: the jobseeker service process changed. The initial interview must be arranged within 10 working days of the start of job search; supplementary discussions are based on service need rather than an automatic fixed cadence. Part-time jobseekers are generally subject to the job-search obligation and current official guidance says they generally apply for four job opportunities per month unless the authority reduces/removes the obligation under the statutory rules. Jobs specifically indicated by the employment authority are binding from the start of unemployment; meeting the ordinary monthly count or publishing a Työnhakuprofiili does not replace an indicated-job task. Source: https://tyomarkkinatori.fi/en/news/tyollisyyspalveluja_koskevat_lakiuudistukset_vuonna_2026_
- 1.3.2026: job-search validity can end after the first failure to deal with the employment authority in the required way and by its deadline. This is separate from the unemployment-security consequence model: a first qualifying lapse can lead to a seven-day unpaid period; a second or later lapse within 12 months can lead to a six-week work requirement. Source: https://tyomarkkinatori.fi/uutiset/tyollisyyspalveluja_koskevat_lakiuudistukset_vuonna_2026_
- 1.5.2026: Kela yleistuki replaced labour market subsidy and basic unemployment allowance. Source: https://www.kela.fi/laskurit
- 1.7.2026: for jobseekers aged 25+, covered open higher-education studies starting on/after 1.7.2026 at an open university, open UAS, `kansalaisopisto` or summer university do not affect unemployment-security entitlement and do not need to be reported for an unemployment-security assessment. Normal job-search duties continue. Under-25 cases and studies outside the rule still require reporting/assessment. Source: https://tyomarkkinatori.fi/uutiset/avoimet-korkeakouluopinnot-eivat-jatkossa-vaikuta-25-vuotta-tayttaneiden-tyottomyysturvaan1
- 1.8.2026: OPH S2 and preparatory-education curriculum changes took effect. Source: https://www.oph.fi/fi/uutiset/2026/perustemuutokset-voimaan-182026-muutoksilla-edistetaan-maahanmuuttotaustaisten
- 1.8.2026: additional learning-support/compulsory-education changes also entered into force; treat school-support pages as high-volatility. Source: https://www.oph.fi/fi/uutiset/2026/esi-ja-perusopetus-kaikki-oppimisen-tuen-uudistukseen-liittyvat-lakimuutokset-astuneet
- 31.8./1.9.2026 seasonal checkpoint: a jobseeker under 25 without vocational education after comprehensive/upper-secondary school can be subject to a separate education-application obligation for unemployment security. For autumn 2026 the published deadline was 31.8.2026; if the person remained an unemployed jobseeker on 1.9., employment services can ask what education they applied to. Failure to apply, causing non-selection, refusing a place or not starting can affect unemployment security when there is no accepted reason. Alternative ways to fulfil the obligation can be agreed in the employment plan in specified circumstances. Do not infer a benefit loss; check the person's plan/Asiointi and route to the authority. Sources: https://tyomarkkinatori.fi/henkiloasiakkaat/tietoa-tyoelamasta/tyottomyysturva/alle-25-vuotiaan-tyottomyysturva and https://tyomarkkinatori.fi/uutiset/yhteishaku-2026-ja-koulutushaun-vaikutukset-tyottomyysturvaan
- 1.9.2026: publishing and keeping a Työnhakuprofiili published becomes generally part of jobseeker obligations where the statutory requirement applies. New jobseekers generally have 15 working days after job search starts. For job searches begun before 1.9., the obligation or an applicable exemption is set at the next job-search discussion or when the plan is prepared; when the discussion triggers the obligation, current guidance gives 15 working days after that discussion. A published profile is valid for a maximum of six months at a time; if job search continues, publication must be renewed so it remains published when required. Statutory exceptions exist. Sources: https://tyomarkkinatori.fi/uutiset/tyonhakuprofiili-tulee-osaksi-tyonhakua-1-9-alkaen and https://tyomarkkinatori.fi/en/instructions-and-support/personal-customers/instructions-for-the-my-job-path-section/independent-job-search/how-to-fill-in-your-job-applicant-profile
- 1.9.2026: Espoo changed walk-in access for employment-service matters. Sello handles urgent official job-search matters without appointment Mon–Fri 9–15; Piispanportti only Mon and Fri 9–15; Iso Omena's Information Point is Wed 9–12 and 13–16 and does not handle official matters. General job-search guidance at Sello and Piispanportti remains Mon–Fri 9–16 and also does not handle official matters. Source: https://www.espoo.fi/en/news/2026/08/changes-opening-hours-jobseekers-advisory-services-1-september-2026
- Citizenship Act changes effective 17.12.2025 continue to affect 2026 applications. Source: https://migri.fi/en/-/amendments-to-citizenship-act-enter-into-force-on-17-december-2025
- 1.1.2027: citizenship-test provisions enter into force; Migri states the new sufficient-knowledge-of-Finnish-society requirement applies to citizenship applications submitted on/after 1.3.2027. Keep as a future watch item, not a current case requirement. Source: https://migri.fi/en/-/finland-to-introduce-citizenship-test-as-changes-to-citizenship-act-take-effect-on-1-january-2027
- Permanent residence permit requirements changed for applications submitted on/after 8.1.2026. Source: https://migri.fi/en/application-for-a-permanent-residence-permit
- Employment services have been municipal employment-area services since 1.1.2025; TE-toimisto is legacy terminology. Source: https://tyomarkkinatori.fi/en/news/uudenmaan-te-toimisto-lakkautetaan-ja-tyonhakijoiden-palvelut-siirtyvat-tyollisyysalueille-1.1.2025
