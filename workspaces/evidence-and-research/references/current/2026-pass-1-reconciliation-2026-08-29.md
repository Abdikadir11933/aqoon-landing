# Pass 1 reconciliation — employment and integration rules

Checked: 29 August 2026. This change record reconciles the earlier AQOON Pass 1 entry-route records with a stronger submitted research ledger and current official primary pages. It adds rule context; it does not convert AQOON into a decision-maker.

## Confirmed additions

- The initial interview is arranged within ten working days from the start of job search under current 2026 guidance.
- Part-time jobseekers generally moved to the four-job-opportunities-per-month rule from 1 January 2026. The individual authority plan remains the controlling instruction.
- The published consequences model is two-tier: a first lapse can lead to a seven-day unpaid period; a repeat lapse within 12 months can require six weeks of qualifying activity before unemployment security resumes.
- The first integration plan is prepared within three years of the first residence permit/residence card or registered right of residence. The first plan can last up to one year; normal total duration is up to two years from signing, with an individual extension of up to two additional years in specified situations.
- A jobseeker's integration plan is prepared by the employment authority; several non-jobseeker pathways, including child-home-care recipients, are municipal pathways. The TYM multisectoral model is a separate coordinated route for people whose employment needs multiple public services.

## Operating guardrail

No generic duty, plan type, sanction, exemption, benefit or deadline is an AQOON decision. Operators must check the person's own authority instruction and route urgent missed-deadline or consequence questions back to the employment authority immediately.
