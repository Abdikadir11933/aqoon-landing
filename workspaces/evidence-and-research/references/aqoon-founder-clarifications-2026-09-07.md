# Founder clarifications — 2026-09-07

Source: founder instructions in the AQOON research conversation, consolidated on 2026-09-07. Status: founder-reported context and intent, not independent validation. Public summary omits personal finances, buyer correspondence and private delivery details.

## Business thesis

The founder's central claim is that useful programmes can exist without being discovered, understood, trusted or successfully entered. AQOON aims to own the connected journey from finding the right people to helping them actually start. The initial wedge is Somali-speaking people in Finland, with wider applicability to be tested.

The current research question is what observed requests reveal about unmet needs, cities, audiences and possible buyers. It is not primarily a CRM response-time or overdue-follow-up audit. Requests across work, adult education and children's schooling are evidence of expressed needs; they do not establish the size of Finland's unserved population or prove that all integration provision is failing.

## Trust and content

The founder reports using faceless videos and emphasizes useful explanation, understandable Somali, familiar ways of speaking, relevant greetings and cultural or religious context. Showing a face and increasing production quality are optional improvements, not necessary conditions for trust. Treat audience preferences as something to learn, not as uniform traits of Somali people.

TikTok is an initial contact channel. Practical guidance, private conversation and face-to-face work can carry the relationship further. A more systematic network of trusted mothers, community connectors and creators is an intended expansion; do not describe that network as already fully operating.

Statements about low effort or no spending refer to the founder's description of recent experiments. Do not extrapolate them to a verified lifetime zero-spend claim. Historical spend, content production methods and exact campaign boundaries require reconciliation before making efficiency claims.

## Record reliability and execution

The founder explicitly says follow-up dates and workflow statuses have not been maintained consistently. Those fields cannot by themselves prove late calls, missed interviews or a 48-hour backlog. Use reliable contact/need aggregates for demand analysis and explicit confirmation or reconciled activity records for execution analysis.

This does not mean execution is irrelevant. The founder also identifies unfinished commitments and limited delivery capacity. Assess those privately using actual commitments and available time, separately from inferred CRM flags.

## Strategic openness

Assess recurring revenue, buyer demand, sales reliability and delivery economics honestly. Explore adjacent commercial partnerships or an owned product as hypotheses where relevant. The current public offer remains the approved baseline until a change is chosen. Neither early traction nor the founder's ambition proves scalable economics or repeatable outcomes.
