## What this suggests

Concrete explanation, relevance and an actionable route appear promising. The support rationale generated many attributed follows; reading support and the walkthrough generated substantial sharing. Different topics serve different jobs, so rank against the intended action rather than views alone. Faceless formats can carry useful explanations; these observations do not isolate the causal effect of language, greeting, timing or visual production.

For future tests, define the topic, target cohort, format, publication date, non-PII campaign tag and intended next action beforehand. Compare the same observation windows and record reliable downstream actions. These results alone do not prove programme attendance, improved integration or commercial return.
