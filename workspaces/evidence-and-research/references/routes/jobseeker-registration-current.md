# Jobseeker registration — current route

## Use this route when

The person wants to start or continue a public-employment-services customer path and says they are unemployed, laid off, or becoming unemployed. It is not a general shortcut for every resident seeking integration information.

## Official route

1. Confirm the municipality and whether the job search is already active.
2. If the person is becoming unemployed or laid off, direct them to their own Työmarkkinatori Asiointi service. The official page says registration may be made before the change and should be made no later than the first unemployment day for someone seeking unemployment security.
3. If the person cannot use the stated online identification methods, or the official nationality guidance directs them to in-person service, resolve the local employment-services contact rather than inventing a workaround.
4. Ask the person to retain the authority's confirmation, assigned tasks and any deadline shown in their own account. The next official discussion and plan are authority-led.

## What happens next

The authority agrees how service proceeds. An employment plan is made with the customer and is revisited in later job-search discussions. In Asiointi, the person can see plan tasks, employment-security information that concerns them, requests for clarification, status changes and contact options. AQOON may translate the sequence into plain language and help prepare facts/questions for the meeting.

## Guardrails

- Being registered is not a confirmed right to unemployment security or a payment.
- AQOON must not click through registration, submit declarations, or say which statutory duties apply to a person.
- Do not route a stay-at-home parent or another non-jobseeker into this path solely to obtain basic integration guidance; use the municipality-specific integration-assessment route where available.
- Benefits, study while unemployed, work trials, training and work-ability support require their own verified route records. They are deliberately out of scope here.
