# S2 and preparatory education from 1.8.2026

OPH changes took effect on 1.8.2026. The appropriateness of S2 is based on the student's actual Finnish/Swedish language skill, not merely the mother-tongue entry in population data. Teachers assess the need and present a recommendation; guardians make the decision on the syllabus choice.

From August 2026, education providers may also offer up to one year of additional preparatory instruction after preparatory education for qualifying pupils; provision is voluntary for the organiser and must be checked locally.
