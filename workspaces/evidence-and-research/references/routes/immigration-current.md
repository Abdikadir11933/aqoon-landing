# Immigration route safety

Citizenship rules changed again for applications submitted on or after 17.12.2025, including stricter sufficient-financial-resources and integrity requirements. Permanent residence permit requirements changed for applications submitted on or after 8.1.2026 and now include multiple application paths.

Primary sources:
- https://migri.fi/en/-/amendments-to-citizenship-act-enter-into-force-on-17-december-2025
- https://migri.fi/en/application-for-a-permanent-residence-permit

Never use the shortcut `5 years + YKI = citizenship`. Verify the current Migri page for every case and let Migri decide.
