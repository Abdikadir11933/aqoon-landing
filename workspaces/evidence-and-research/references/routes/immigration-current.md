# Immigration route safety

Citizenship rules changed again for applications submitted on or after 17.12.2025, including stricter sufficient-financial-resources and integrity requirements. A further citizenship-test requirement is scheduled to apply to applications submitted on or after 1.3.2027; keep this as a future watch item until Migri's live application guidance is updated.

Permanent residence permit requirements changed for applications submitted on or after 8.1.2026. The standard six-year route requires continuous/Brexit-permit residence, two years of Finnish work history and satisfactory Finnish or Swedish skills (B1, with the stated age exception). Separate four-year application paths exist; their exact conditions must be checked from Migri's current path page for each case. Do not repeat the retired shortcut “four years of continuous residence” as a general rule.
