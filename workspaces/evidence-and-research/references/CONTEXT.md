# Canonical Public Knowledge

This is the canonical public knowledge layer. Records here require source IDs, scope, verification state and volatility.

Subfolders:
- `authorities/`: who decides/provides public services
- `sources/`: official/provider source registry and link bank
- `services/`: reusable service facts
- `routes/`: current navigation logic
- `calculators/`: official estimator tools and limitations
- `current/`: dated cross-domain changes that affect multiple routes

Live programme availability does not belong here as a timeless fact; verify from the current provider source at research time.
