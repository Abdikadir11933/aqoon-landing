## 4. Current thesis to test

Working hypothesis:

> A specific useful problem/opportunity acquires the family. A structured first interview then reveals whether the family has additional legitimate service-navigation needs, and AQOON can match and follow those needs to action/outcome.

Required fields are now collected in the first interview:
- acquisition source;
- prior awareness;
- ability to self-navigate;
- access barrier(s);
- additional needs discovered;
- follow-up permission;
- route-specific proof fields for hobbies/Vantaa and daycare/private-daycare decisions.

Primary business metric under test: **confirmed additional needs discovered per acquired family**, alongside cost per identifiable contact, first-interview completion, action rate, verified outcome rate and repeat-contact rate.

## 5. Cost claim warning

Founder reported only a few euros of recent TikTok promotion. Organic and promoted exposure are not cleanly separated in the current evidence snapshot. Therefore do not divide the full lead count by that spend and present the result as a paid CPA. Paid acquisition cost must use campaign-level spend and attributable conversions from the same cohort.
