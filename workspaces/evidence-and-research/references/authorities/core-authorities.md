# Core Authority Registry

## authority.kela
Kela. National social-security institution. Canonical site: https://www.kela.fi/

## authority.migri
Finnish Immigration Service. Residence permits and citizenship. Canonical site: https://migri.fi/

## authority.oph
Finnish National Agency for Education. National education guidance, curricula and YKI. Canonical site: https://www.oph.fi/

## authority.employment-area
Municipal employment areas provide public employment services from 1.1.2025. National digital service: https://tyomarkkinatori.fi/

## authority.municipality
Municipalities are competent for their own local services such as early childhood education and many integration/employment routes. Always resolve the municipality/city before using a local route.

## authority.vantaa-employment
Vantaa employment and integration services. Provides the local employment-authority/customer route for Vantaa residents, including jobseeker and integration-plan service paths. Canonical site: https://www.vantaa.fi/fi/tyonhaku-ja-tyollistaminen . It does not decide Kela payments, residence permits, school places or provider admission.

## authority.finlex
Finlex is the official source for Finnish legislation: https://www.finlex.fi/

## authority.tem
Ministry of Economic Affairs and Employment (Työ- ja elinkeinoministeriö). Employment and enterprise policy, including recruitment-support measures. Canonical site: https://tem.fi/

## authority.okm
Ministry of Education and Culture (Opetus- ja kulttuuriministeriö). Education, culture and national physical-activity/movement programme funding. Canonical site: https://okm.fi/

## authority.stm
Ministry of Social Affairs and Health (Sosiaali- ja terveysministeriö). Social welfare and health policy and organisation-funding decisions, including STEA grant decisions. Canonical site: https://stm.fi/

## authority.stea
Funding Centre for Social Welfare and Health Organisations (STEA). Prepares and administers social welfare/health organisation grants on STM's behalf. Canonical site: https://www.stea.fi/

## authority.ely-centre
Centres for Economic Development, Transport and the Environment (ELY-keskukset). Regional coordination of EU structural-fund (ESR+/EAKR) calls via EURA2021. Canonical site: https://www.ely-keskus.fi/
