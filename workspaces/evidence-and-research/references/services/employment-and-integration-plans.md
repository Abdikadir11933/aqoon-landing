# Employment and integration plans

Jobseeker registration opens the employment-services customer path. The employment authority makes an employment plan with the customer in the initial interview or a preceding supplementary job-search discussion, and it is monitored in later discussions. The plan can state the person's work-search goals, agreed services and how they will deal with employment services. A person in an integration period may be offered an integration plan based on the competence/integration service-needs assessment. The exact plan, services, obligations and timing are decided by the responsible authority, not AQOON.

The national integration page says that the assessment covers, among other things, competence, work/functional capacity, conditions for work, study or integration in another way, and needs for training or integration-promoting services. A resulting integration plan can include integration training, job-search or career coaching, work trials, Finnish/Swedish or mother-tongue study, or literacy teaching. These are examples of possible plan content, not an AQOON promise that a person is entitled to a particular service.


An integration plan is prepared when the official assessment identifies a need. The employment authority prepares it for an immigrant registered as an unemployed jobseeker; the municipality prepares it for several non-jobseeker groups, including people receiving child home care allowance and people receiving social assistance on a non-temporary basis. The first plan must be prepared within three years of the first residence permit/residence card or registration of right of residence. Its content and duration are individual: the first plan can last up to one year; as a rule, the total duration does not exceed two years from the first plan's signing, with a possible individual extension of up to two further years for specific integration measures. AQOON must not calculate an individual's deadline, duration or plan type without current authority confirmation.

For long-term unemployed people needing coordinated employment, health, social or rehabilitation support, the authority may use the multisectoral promotion-of-employment model (TYM). It is a separate coordinated authority route, not a diagnosis or service AQOON can assign. AQOON may explain the model and help the person prepare questions for their authority.

For Vantaa, the local page says the integration plan is offered to jobseekers in the integration period based on that assessment. Vantaa separately states that its competence/integration service-needs assessment is for people outside the labour force, including for example a parent caring for a child at home, a pensioner, a person unable to work or a person granted international protection. Do not assume that a stay-at-home parent has the same employment-services route as an active jobseeker, and do not tell a person to register as a jobseeker solely to access basic integration guidance.

AQOON operator questions: municipality; whether job search is active and its start date; whether an initial interview/assessment has happened; whether a plan exists and its next agreed action; work/study goal; language/education need; childcare/availability; and whether the person is being served as a jobseeker or outside the labour force. These collect facts for navigation, not a benefit decision.
