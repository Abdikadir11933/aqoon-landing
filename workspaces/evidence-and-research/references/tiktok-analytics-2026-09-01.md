# TikTok analytics snapshot - 1 Sep 2026

Status: **AQOON primary evidence / account and content-performance snapshot**

Use: internal claim-checking, content planning and aggregate buyer reporting. This is not a permanent benchmark, an outcome study or proof of paid acquisition efficiency.

## Scope and provenance

This snapshot combines:

- a TikTok Studio screen recording reviewed as 260 chronological one-second frames, with full-resolution checks around each analytics screen;
- the owner-supplied TikTok `Overview.xlsx` export covering 1 Sep 2025 through 31 Aug 2026;
- account and video screens visible in TikTok Studio on 1-2 Sep 2026.

The export is authoritative for exact daily account totals. The recording is the source for follower demographics, activity, traffic sources, search terms and individual-video analytics. TikTok abbreviates some values with `K`; those values remain approximate below. Captions were truncated in Studio, so topic names are descriptive labels rather than complete caption transcripts.

This snapshot does not replace the dated 27 and 28 Aug launch-funnel snapshots. Those records have different observation windows and measure identifiable contacts and operational progression rather than account-wide content performance.

## Performance interpretation

### Strongest observed content jobs

1. **Rights and opportunity discovery converts followers.** Video 3 produced 326 new followers from 8,829 plays, about 3.7% of plays. It also produced the highest average watch time, about 17.14 seconds, and 157 favorites.
2. **Parent-rights content combines reach, trust and action signals.** Video 5 produced about 11K plays, 598 likes, 139 shares, 113 favorites and 159 new followers.
3. **A specific child or school problem is highly shareable.** Video 1 produced the largest visible share count, 232, and the largest visible play count, 12K, despite a 3.2% full-watch rate.
4. **Urgent reminders can improve completion.** Video 7 had the highest full-watch rate, 14.2%, but fewer new followers per play than the broader rights and opportunity explanations.
5. **Procedural explainers play a middle or bottom-funnel role.** The daycare-question video reached 8,769 plays but produced 82 likes, 18 shares, 17 favorites and 18 followers. Reach alone does not show whether viewers later completed an action.

### Retention constraint

The largest loss occurred in the first one or two seconds across every visible retention screen. The useful subject matter can perform after viewers remain, but the opening needs to identify the exact person, benefit or problem immediately.

### Discovery constraint

For You supplied 77.0-94.3% of traffic across the inspected videos. Search reached at most 3.2% in the visible set. The Wilma video was unusually profile-led at 20.8%, suggesting evergreen profile browsing, but the recording does not show a per-video profile-visit count or conversion.

## What this evidence proves

Using the evidence hierarchy in `../CONTEXT.md`, this snapshot supports the following:

- AQOON has a real founder-led distribution asset with 829 followers and 123,509 exported annual views at the observation point.
- Useful Somali-language content reaches a predominantly Finland-based female audience aged 25-44.
- Rights, opportunities, child/school problems and actionable local-service information generate the strongest observed combinations of watch time, sharing, saving and following.
- Recommendation distribution reaches beyond existing followers; most visible traffic comes from For You and most video viewers are non-followers.
- The first one to two seconds are the clearest repeat content-performance constraint.

The separate 27 and 28 Aug funnel snapshots support a stronger downstream claim: TikTok-attributed content created identifiable, voluntarily submitted help requests. Those snapshots must be cited for contact counts because this analytics snapshot does not contain CRM or outcome data.

## What this evidence does not prove

- A view, profile view, like, share, favorite or follower is not an identifiable contact, interview, application, start or persisted outcome.
- Account-wide engagement cannot be converted into a paid cost per lead because promoted and organic exposure are not separated by matched campaign and cohort.
- Per-video profile visits and profile-visit conversion were not shown.
- Reposts were not shown at account or video level.
- Account-wide favorites were not shown.
- Video 10's date and engagement counters were not visible.
- The activity screen does not prove midnight is the best posting time across days.
- An older video is not current proof that a service, price, deadline, right or application route remains unchanged.
- The snapshot does not show that every follower is a parent, that every viewer lives in Finland or that the audience represents all Somali-speaking people in Finland.

## Business and content implications

- Use one concrete problem or opportunity as the acquisition door, then route the person to one useful next action.
- Repeat the proven topic structure: specific right or opportunity, who it concerns, main misunderstanding, concrete next step.
- Put the benefit in the first on-screen sentence. Do not open with a logo, broad greeting or long setup.
- Use exact Finnish service terms together with Somali explanation, especially `päiväkoti`, `Wilma`, municipality and service names, while verifying volatile claims before publication.
- Treat high-share/high-save videos as source material for focused sequences and evergreen Caawi guidance, not as permission to copy an old service claim unchanged.
- Measure each future asset beyond views with a non-PII `utm_campaign` ID mapped to the governed campaign record and the runtime fields `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign` and `referrer_host`.
- Keep family and buyer CTAs separate. Family-facing content normally routes to `aqoon.live/caawi` or the relevant `/caawi/<topic>` page.
- Do not increase top-of-funnel output beyond the operators' capacity to answer, interview and move contacts to action or outcome.
