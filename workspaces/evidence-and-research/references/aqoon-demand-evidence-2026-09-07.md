## Interpretation

Observed: requests span several needs and five cities, with TikTok attribution on most recorded needs. This supports the narrower claim that the current route can elicit concrete requests beyond one service topic.

Inference to test: relevant explanations and a trusted, simple contact route may reveal demand missed by conventional communication. Possible buyer conversations can be organized around the specific need and entry barrier, rather than views alone.

Not established: total addressable market, a representative city distribution, verified eligibility, provider capacity, incremental causal impact, repeat purchase, cost per acquisition or profitable delivery. A request is neither an application nor a verified start. Do not divide these totals by unrelated account views to claim a conversion rate.
