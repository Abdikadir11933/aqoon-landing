# Programmes and coaching cheat sheet

Updated 2026-08-27. These are services, not jobs. Most require active jobseeker status and authority agreement.

## Työnhakuvalmennus
**What it is:** Coaching to learn independent job-search skills: channels, CV/applications and how to evaluate suitable vacancies.

**Criteria:** Registered jobseeker + authority assesses need + agreed in employment plan.

**Paid?** No salary from the service.

**How to start:** Ask local employment authority whether it can be added to plan.

**Next:** Client becomes better able to search and apply independently.

**Ask:** Is the barrier CV, applications, job-search channels or not knowing where to apply?

## Työhönvalmennus
**What it is:** Personal hands-on support to find work or an apprenticeship and support the job start.

**Criteria:** Registered jobseeker + authority assessment + agreed in plan.

**Paid?** No salary from coaching itself.

**Next:** Coach can help find real jobs/oppisopimus, applications, interviews, contract steps and settling into work.

**Ask:** Does the client already know the field and mainly need someone to help all the way to employers?

## Uravalmennus
**What it is:** Career coaching for choosing a field, changing career, returning to work or considering entrepreneurship.

**Criteria:** Registered jobseeker + authority assessment + plan.

**Paid?** No salary from coaching.

**Ask:** Is the main problem that the client does not know which job/field to aim for?

## Ammatinvalinta- ja uraohjaus
**What it is:** Guidance to explore profession/career choices and suitability; exact local service can vary.

**Paid?** Not a job.

**How to start:** Ask local employment services/guidance service.

**Ask:** What has the client done before? What do they not want? What strengths/interests do they identify?

**Watch out:** Do not infer health/disability or other sensitive facts. Only use such information if the client chooses to discuss it and it is relevant.

## When they say no to a programme
Ask why:
- too basic / too advanced?
- wants paid work instead?
- childcare?
- travel?
- daytime schedule impossible?
- language level wrong?
- does not understand what the programme is?
