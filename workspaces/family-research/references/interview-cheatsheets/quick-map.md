# Quick map - which door opens what?

Updated 2026-08-27.

## If unemployed
1. Register / keep job search active in Työmarkkinatori.
2. This can open unemployment-benefit processing and access to employment-service plans and services.
3. Check yleistuki / unemployment fund, housing support, and whether an employment or integration plan exists.
4. Then match the actual barrier: job search, skills, Finnish, experience, childcare, transport or qualification.

## If the client says “I want work”
- Direct job first if realistic.
- Palkkatuki = paid job; subsidy goes to employer.
- Oppisopimus = paid job + vocational learning.
- Työkokeilu = no salary; unemployment benefit may continue; employment authority must agree first.
- Työhönvalmennus = hands-on help finding a job or apprenticeship.

## If childcare blocks work/study
- Check child age + municipality + required start date + care hours.
- Municipal early childhood education: normally apply at least 4 months before need.
- Sudden unforeseen work/study need can allow faster processing; evidence is required and city rules vary.
- Also check private daycare/service voucher, family daycare, kotihoidon tuki, or yksityisen hoidon tuki depending on situation.

## If a letter/application is the problem
WHO sent it? -> WHAT document? -> DATE -> DEADLINE -> REQUIRED ACTION -> REQUIRED ATTACHMENTS -> WHAT OPENS NEXT?

Do not translate only. Identify the action pathway.
