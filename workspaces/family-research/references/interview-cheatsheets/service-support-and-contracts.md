# Service support, decisions and contracts cheat sheet

Last reviewed: 2026-08-27.

Use when the person brings a letter, decision, bill, contract or says “I don't understand what they want.”

## Universal 7-question triage

1. **Who sent it?** Kela, Migri, municipality, employment services, school/daycare, landlord, insurer, telecom/electricity company, debt collection, other.
2. **What is it?** application, request for information, decision, invoice, contract, reminder, termination/change notice, appeal instruction.
3. **Date?** When issued/received.
4. **Deadline?** Reply, document, payment, appeal or cancellation deadline.
5. **What has already been done?** application/contact/payment/response.
6. **What does the client want?** understand, respond, apply, challenge, cancel, compare, pay, contact authority.
7. **What documents are available?** Never copy unnecessary personal data into public GitHub/reusable knowledge.

## Employment-related “sopimus” distinctions

- **Työsopimus:** real employment contract; salary and employment rights/obligations.
- **Oppisopimus:** paid employment contract + vocational education plan.
- **Koulutussopimus:** student workplace-learning arrangement; no employment relationship/salary.
- **Työkokeilu agreement:** employment-service trial arrangement; not an employment contract and no salary.

If a client says “I only want the paid version,” distinguish these before recording the answer.

## Consumer contracts

For electricity, phone/broadband, insurance and other consumer contracts, first identify provider, contract type, fixed/ongoing term, price, notice/cancellation terms, and whether the issue is a sale/renewal/dispute. Use KKV/FINE/Traficom or the relevant official/independent source for rights questions rather than guessing from the bill.

Useful starting points:
- KKV contracts: https://www.kkv.fi/en/consumer-affairs/contracts/
- KKV Consumer Advisory: https://www.kkv.fi/en/consumer-affairs/consumer-advisory-services/
- Traficom telephone/broadband: https://www.traficom.fi/en/telephone-and-broadband/telephone-and-broadband-users/subscriber-connection-agreement
- FINE insurance/financial advice: https://www.fine.fi/en
- Energy Authority electricity comparison: https://www.sahkonhinta.fi/

## Never do from a first interview alone

- promise an appeal will succeed
- tell the client a contract is legally invalid without checking terms/law
- promise an authority/provider will cancel a fee/debt
- interpret immigration/right-to-work status without current Migri/authority evidence
- store the person's letter/decision text in reusable public knowledge
