# Forms, letters, applications and service support

Updated 2026-08-27. Do not stop at translation; identify the action the document requires.

## Universal document check
1. WHO sent it? Kela / city / school / Migri / employment services / private provider.
2. WHAT is it? Application / request for information / decision / invoice / appointment / appeal instruction.
3. DATE + DEADLINE.
4. WHAT must client do?
5. WHAT attachments are required?
6. WHAT happens if they do nothing?
7. WHAT door opens after this action?

## Lisäselvityspyyntö
**What it is:** Request for additional information/documents because authority cannot decide case yet.

**Criteria:** The request itself tells what is missing and deadline.

**Paid?** Not money.

**How to respond:** Provide exactly requested information through stated channel before deadline.

**Next:** Authority continues processing after sufficient information is received.

**Ask:** What is requested word-for-word? Deadline? Is document available? Who can issue proof?

**Watch out:** Do not treat it as a rejection.

## Päätös + muutoksenhaku / valitusohje
**What it is:** Formal decision plus instructions for correction/appeal where available.

**How to act:** Read decision reason, effective dates and appeal instructions. Use stated official route.

**Next:** Accept/act on decision or submit correction/appeal with grounds/evidence.

**Ask:** What was decided? Why? From what date? Deadline? Which part is wrong?

**Watch out:** Never invent an appeal deadline - read actual decision/current authority guidance.

## Hakemus
**What it is:** Application that starts a formal process. Many services/benefits do not begin automatically because person registered elsewhere.

**How to apply:** Official e-service where possible; otherwise correct paper/PDF form. Save confirmation/receipt.

**Next:** Authority/provider checks criteria and may request more information before deciding.

**Ask:** Which application? Sent? When? Receipt? Attachments? Current status in OmaKela/Edlevo/Työmarkkinatori/other system?

## Common letter words to identify
- lisäselvityspyyntö
- päätös
- määräaika
- valitusohje / muutoksenhaku
- maksupäivä
- ajanvaraus / appointment
- puuttuva liite

## Key operator habit
A lot of “I need help with Kela/school/employment services” cases reduce to one missing document, deadline or action. Identify that first.
