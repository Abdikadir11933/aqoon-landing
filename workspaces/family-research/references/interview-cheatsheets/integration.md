# Integration and language cheat sheet

Updated 2026-08-27.

## Kotoutumissuunnitelma
**What it is:** Integration plan with the relevant authority. It can connect language learning, employment, education and other integration steps.

**Criteria:** Depends on integration-service assessment and the person’s situation/status. Authority decides route.

**Paid?** The plan itself is not money.

**How to start:** Ask local integration/employment services for assessment and plan if person is in scope.

**What it can open:** agreed integration training, language study, employment services or other suitable steps.

**Ask:** Onko tehty kotoutumisen palvelutarpeen arviointi? Onko kotoutumissuunnitelma voimassa? Mitä siinä sovittiin?

**Watch out:** Do not assume every immigrant automatically has a current integration plan.

## Kotoutumiskoulutus
**What it is:** Integration training commonly combines Finnish/Swedish, working-life skills, society knowledge and study/work preparation.

**Criteria:** Usually based on authority assessment and plan; exact provider, level and route vary locally.

**Paid?** Training itself is normally free when organised as labour-market training. Unemployment benefit may continue and expense allowance may be possible.

**How to start:** Through integration/employment services and agreed plan, often after language/skills assessment.

**Ask:** What level? Day/evening? Childcare? Travel? Was previous course too easy/hard? Would vocational Finnish fit better?

## YKI
**What it is:** National Certificate of Language Proficiency used for purposes such as citizenship, work or study.

**Criteria:** Correct language and level for the purpose. For citizenship or regulated purposes, verify the current accepted proof from Migri/OPH.

**Paid?** Test has a fee; it is not a paid programme.

**How to start:** Register for an available YKI test through the official OPH route when registration opens.

**Ask:** Why is YKI needed - citizenship, work, study or professional requirement? Current level? Test already booked?

**Watch out:** Never promise that one specific score/result automatically satisfies another authority’s requirement without checking the current rule.
