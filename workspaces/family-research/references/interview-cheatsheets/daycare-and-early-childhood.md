# Daycare and early childhood cheat sheet

Updated 2026-08-27. City-specific rules, fees and urgent-processing evidence must be rechecked live.

## Kunnallinen varhaiskasvatus / päiväkoti
**What it is:** Municipal early childhood education organised by the municipality.

**Criteria:** Municipality/residence and service conditions. Apply where care is needed.

**Paid?** Usually client fee based on family income/size and care arrangement; not automatically free.

**How to apply:** Municipality e-service. Normal planning rule: apply at least 4 months before place is needed.

**Next:** Municipality makes placement decision; preferred daycare is not guaranteed.

**Ask:** Child age? Kotikunta? Start date? Care hours? 1-3 preferred locations? Already applied?

## Äkillinen / ennakoimaton varhaiskasvatuksen tarve
**What it is:** Faster processing when genuine sudden and unforeseen work/study creates urgent childcare need.

**Criteria:** Exact proof rules vary. Operator PDF notes Helsinki/Espoo currently describe applying as soon as possible but at least 2 weeks before need with evidence.

**Paid?** Same fee system as granted daycare place.

**How to apply:** Use city urgent route/marking and attach proof from employer/study provider; where required, evidence that other guardians cannot provide care.

**Next:** Municipality searches available place; it may be farther away.

**Ask:** What changed suddenly? When did client get notice? Written proof? Can other guardians care for child?

**Watch out:** End of parental leave or simply moving municipality may not count as sudden/unforeseen; city rules vary.

## Perhepäivähoito
**What it is:** Early childhood education in smaller family-daycare setting, municipal or private depending on provider.

**Paid?** Fee depends on municipal/private model.

**How to start:** Choose/search this option where municipality/provider offers it.

**Ask:** Would a smaller group fit? Normal daytime or shift-care need?

## Palveluseteli
**What it is:** Municipality-funded voucher toward an approved private early childhood education provider where municipality offers it.

**Criteria:** Municipality-specific approved providers and rules.

**Paid?** Family usually pays a client portion; not automatically free private daycare.

**How to apply:** Municipality + approved provider process.

**Next:** Voucher decision + private provider place/contract.

**Ask:** Is private daycare okay? Maximum affordable fee? Is preferred provider approved for service voucher?

**Watch out:** Kela private-care support is generally not paid for a private daycare place using municipal service voucher.

## Kotihoidon tuki
**What it is:** Kela support when a young child is cared for at home instead of a municipal early childhood education place.

**Criteria:** Youngest eligible child over parental-allowance period and under 3; no municipal ECEC place; other Kela conditions apply.

**Paid?** Yes - Kela benefit to family.

**How to apply:** OmaKela (paper WH1 if needed).

**Ask:** Youngest under 3? Municipal place? Who cares for child? Parental allowance still being paid?

**Watch out:** This can interact with employment/integration choices; do not assume staying home is best route.

## Yksityisen hoidon tuki
**What it is:** Kela support for eligible private early childhood education/private caregiver.

**Criteria:** Under-school-age child in eligible private care; not municipal ECEC and generally not service-voucher daycare.

**Paid?** Yes - Kela pays support to private provider/caregiver; family pays remaining fee.

**How to apply:** OmaKela; provider supplies childcare information.

**Ask:** Private place already found? Service voucher or not? Fee? Is caregiver/provider eligible?

## Esiopetus
**What it is:** Pre-primary education before school, separate from additional daycare hours.

**Paid?** Pre-primary education itself is free. Additional ECEC around it can have fees.

**How to start:** Municipality’s pre-primary enrolment/application process; timing/system city-specific.

**Next:** Placement + schedule; additional daycare may require separate application.

**Ask:** Only esiopetus or esiopetus + täydentävä varhaiskasvatus?

**Watch out:** Pre-primary placement does not automatically cover parent’s full workday.
