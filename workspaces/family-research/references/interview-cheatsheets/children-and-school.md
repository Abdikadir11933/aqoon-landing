# Children, daycare, school and hobbies cheat sheet

Last reviewed: 2026-08-27.

This category is intentionally route-first rather than one national answer because municipality, age, school and current provider matter heavily. Verify the exact current city/provider page before giving deadlines, fees, availability or entitlement details.

## Daycare / varhaiskasvatus interview

Ask:
- child's exact age
- registered municipality
- desired start date
- required days/hours (day/evening/night/weekend)
- already applied? date?
- current place/queue?
- municipal/private/service voucher/family daycare acceptable?
- sudden/unforeseen need or ordinary planned need?
- work/study reason and proof if an urgent route is being considered
- fee sensitivity

**Do not promise:** a specific placement date, exact fee, urgent place, private service-voucher eligibility, or a specific daycare before verifying the municipality/provider.

Useful official starting points:
- Helsinki: https://www.hel.fi/en/childhood-and-education/early-childhood-education/applying-for-early-childhood-education
- Vantaa: https://www.vantaa.fi/en/education-and-learning/early-childhood-education/applying-early-childhood-education
- Espoo: https://www.espoo.fi/en/childcare-and-education/early-childhood-education/applying-municipal-early-childhood-education
- Tampere: https://www.tampere.fi/en/early-childhood-education-and-pre-primary-education/early-childhood-education/applying-and-terminating-place-early-childhood-education-and-care/applying-early-childhood-education

## School support interview

Ask:
- exact age + grade
- municipality + school
- born in Finland / time in Finnish education where relevant
- current Finnish-for-learning level
- current route (preparatory/basic/esiopetus/TUVA etc.)
- S2 status
- support already tried
- meetings/assessments/decisions already made
- next deadline/transition
- what the parent wants to happen next

**Do not diagnose** a learning difficulty or promise S2/special support. AQOON can explain current rules, help prepare questions and help the parent contact the school.

## Hobbies

Ask age/grade, school/neighbourhood, interests, free/low-fee requirement, days/time, travel distance, language, accessibility/support and whether registration has already been attempted.

Always check current registration/open-place status; old programme lists are discovery only.
