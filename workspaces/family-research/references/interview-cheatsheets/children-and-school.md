# School and child support cheat sheet

Updated 2026-08-27. Learning-support terminology changed from 1 August 2025; use current terms.

## S2 - suomi toisena kielenä
**What it is:** Finnish as a second language and literature syllabus for pupils whose Finnish proficiency requires it.

**Criteria:** School assesses language proficiency and educational need; immigrant background alone does not determine it.

**Paid?** Public education support, not money.

**How to start:** Discuss with school/teacher; ask what syllabus child follows and why.

**Ask:** Is child in S2? How long in Finland/Finnish school? What does teacher say about language level?

**Watch out:** Do not say every multilingual child automatically belongs in S2.

## Ryhmäkohtaiset tukimuodot
**What it is:** Current group-level learning support such as general remedial teaching, language-of-instruction remedial teaching and special-teacher teaching alongside other teaching.

**Paid?** School support, not money.

**How to start:** Parent raises concern with teacher/school and asks what support is already being provided.

**Ask:** What has already been tried? How often? Has effect been evaluated?

## Oppilaskohtaiset tukitoimet
**What it is:** Individual support when group-level support is not enough or regular individual measures are needed. Can include special-teacher teaching, small group/special class, interpreting/assistant services or aids.

**Criteria:** Need is assessed; parent/pupil can raise concern. Individual measures require assessment, plan and administrative decision.

**How to start:** Ask school to assess support need and clarify assessment, plan and decision.

**Ask:** Has assessment been done? Plan? Written decision? What does decision say?

**Watch out:** Do not promise “special education”. Help parent request/understand assessment and decision.

## Valmistava opetus
**What it is:** Preparatory education for pupils who need support transitioning into Finnish/Swedish basic education.

**Criteria:** Local education provider assesses suitability and placement.

**Paid?** Public education, not paid programme.

**How to start:** Contact municipality/school education guidance, especially after recent arrival.

**Ask:** When did child arrive in Finland? Has school started? Current teaching group/route?

## Always collect in school cases
- child age and grade
- school + municipality
- time in Finnish education
- Finnish level for learning
- S2 status
- support already tried
- meetings/assessment/decision
- what parent wants to happen next
