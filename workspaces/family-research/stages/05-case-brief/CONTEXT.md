# 05 Case Brief

## Inputs

Evaluated matches and supporting current evidence.

## Process

Turn the evaluation into an understandable private case brief. Preserve caveats and select a feasible next action instead of listing unexplained options.

## Outputs

What appears to fit, why, blockers/unknowns, exact next action, exact official/provider source, deadline if current, documents/questions to prepare and a backup route. State who decides the outcome.
