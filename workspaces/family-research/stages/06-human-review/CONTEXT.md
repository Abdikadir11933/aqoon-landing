# 06 Human Review

## Inputs

The private case brief and its source/evaluation record.

## Process

Audit for unsupported promises, wrong authority, stale dates/sources, wrong city, irrelevant matches, missing blockers and unclear next actions. Human review is the final checkpoint before consequential family advice.

## Outputs

A reviewed brief with reviewer/date and remaining confirmation needs, or a clear list of corrections needed before advice. Do not imply that review happened when it is pending. Keep the case record private.
