# 04 Match Evaluation

## Inputs

Candidates, current source criteria and private case facts.

## Process

Compare each candidate's verified criteria with known facts. Keep missing information and contradictions visible; AQOON does not make the provider's decision.

## Outputs

Exactly one status per candidate: `confirmed-match`, `possible-must-confirm`, or `does-not-fit`, with matched, missing and conflicting criteria, decision maker, source IDs and verification time.
