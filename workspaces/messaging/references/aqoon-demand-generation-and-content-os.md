# AQOON demand generation, outreach and content operating system

Status: canonical operating model from 2 September 2026.

Use this document for every acquisition campaign, outreach partnership, public guide, social post, video idea, script, creator collaboration, community distribution plan and performance review. It governs family-facing demand generation and the separate buyer-facing evidence path.

This system adapts useful ideas from leading marketing frameworks, but AQOON's business model, trust boundaries, evidence rules and observed family behaviour take precedence. AQOON is not a creator business that happens to sell help. Content and distribution are inputs to a measured demand-to-outcome service.

## 1. The one engine

AQOON finds a real need or opportunity that people are missing, explains it through a trusted channel, gives one easy voluntary way to ask for help and owns the uncertain path to a verified next action and outcome.

`real need/opportunity -> relevant trusted reach -> useful explanation -> consented contact -> first interview -> verified route -> assisted action -> partner handoff when relevant -> verified outcome/persistence -> PII-free learning -> better reach and service`

The content funnel and the family journey are connected, but they are not the same thing. Public content may create attention and a request for help. Qualification, matching, application support, handoff and outcome follow-up are operational work.

The system is not strictly linear. A person with an urgent, clear need may move directly from first exposure to `aqoon.live/caawi` or a private call. Funnel stages describe the job to be done, not gates the family must be forced through.

## 2. Two audiences, two promises

Every asset must name one primary audience before production.

### Family or adult

Promise: useful, free, low-friction help to understand an option, find the right route and complete the next useful action.

- Lead with the person's concrete problem or opportunity, not a list of everything AQOON does.
- Give useful help even when no paid partner match exists.
- Never imply eligibility, approval, a place, a benefit, a job or another authority/provider decision.
- Disclose a paid provider relationship before a recommendation or handoff.
- Abducadir is the public founder and principal face. Team capability may be described without making another team member a public face by default.

### Buyer or partner

Promise: measurable acquisition, qualification, conversion, onboarding, persistence and aggregate learning through one core implementation (**Jatkuva tavoittamisen ja palvelun aloituksen toteutus**), the optional **Asiakaspolun ja materiaalien kehitys** add-on and the separate **Henkilöstön valmennus** offer.

- Sell outcomes, an operated journey, training and PII-free evidence.
- Do not sell raw family identities, phone lists or undisclosed access to a community.
- Do not use the family-facing account as a stream of corporate advertisements.
- A buyer proof asset must distinguish observed evidence, current operational state and the next hypothesis to test.

Community connectors, järjestöt and creators can help AQOON reach people. They are not automatically buyers, referrers or representatives of the whole community.

### Surface rule

The audiences share one business engine but never share an undifferentiated landing surface:

| Audience | Canonical owned surface | Search intent | Default conversion |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Family/adult | `aqoon.live/caawi` and `aqoon.live/caawi/<topic>` | practical Somali help with work, education, children, Kela, contracts and Finnish services | topic page or social/community entry -> `/caawi` intake -> private follow-up |
| Buyer/partner | `aqoon.live` and the established Finnish B2B pages | how to reach, serve, integrate, convert, onboard and retain Somali-speaking or otherwise missed communities | relevant B2B evidence/method page -> scoped buyer conversation |

Do not route family search traffic to the B2B root by default. Do not route buyer-intent search traffic into the family intake. `/so/*` is a legacy redirect namespace, not a third surface.

## 3. What a lead means

Do not collapse these levels into one number:

1. **Anonymous reach:** a view, impression, visitor or field exposure.
2. **Engagement:** a share, save, comment, profile visit, link click or direct question without a reliably contactable person.
3. **Identifiable contact:** a valid phone number, DM thread or other voluntary contact route AQOON can use for the stated help request.
4. **Completed intake:** the short request was submitted and a CRM family lead exists.
5. **Qualified need:** the first interview confirmed the situation and route-critical facts. A family may have more than one separately confirmed need.
6. **Verified match:** current published criteria appear to match the known facts, subject to the authority/provider decision.
7. **Assisted action:** an application, registration, document preparation, contact or other agreed step was actually started.
8. **Partner handoff:** the right separate consent was obtained and the agreed minimum information was transferred.
9. **Verified outcome:** the application, registration, start, service, job, study place or other agreed result was confirmed with evidence.
10. **Persistence:** the outcome remained active at the agreed follow-up point.
11. **Returning relationship:** the person voluntarily returns with another Finnish-system question or permits clearly relevant future contact.

A person is never a "lead magnet." The useful answer, opportunity, checklist, explanation, tool or first step is the entry value. It can open a trusted family relationship.

## 4. The complete acquisition map

AQOON uses the channel that already has the relevant person's attention and trust. TikTok is one proven channel, not the offer and not the whole distribution system.

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Founder-led social | `@waalidsupport`, TikTok, selectively cross-posted Reels or Shorts | discovery, relevance, explanation and founder trust | `/caawi` or one intentionally selected private contact route | new viewers, shares, identifiable contacts and completed intakes by asset |
| Caawi family SEO | `aqoon.live/caawi/<topic>` | high-intent Somali discovery, useful explanation and route clarity | topic page -> `/caawi` intake | search entry, intake and downstream outcome |
| AQOON B2B SEO | `aqoon.live`, `havainnot/`, `menetelma/`, `tapaus/` and other established B2B routes | help buyers find AQOON when searching for Somali integration, trusted reach, multilingual navigation, conversion, onboarding or persistence | relevant proof/method page -> scoped buyer conversation | qualified buyer visit, conversation, proposal and decision |

| Trusted community connectors | impactful mothers, known Somali people in Finland and other trusted individuals | warm introduction, relevance and social proof | family contacts AQOON directly through a tagged link/route | source-attributed contacts, interviews and outcomes |
| Järjestöt and community actors | associations, community organisations, hosts and other already trusted/reachable actors | distribution, hosting, explanation and referral | co-branded or AQOON-owned contact route with clear responsibility | cohort reach, contact, progression and aggregate barriers |

The exact Yellow Card URL, ownership and redirect role are not currently documented in the repository. Use `yellow_card_domain` only as an attribution label where that source is genuinely used; do not invent a URL, treat it as the Caawi canonical home or change routing until the owner confirms it.

### Channel selection rule

Choose channels in this order:

1. Where does the intended person already pay attention or ask for help?
2. Who already has legitimate trust with that person?
3. What level of explanation is needed before action?
4. Can AQOON answer and operate the demand created?
5. Can the source and downstream outcome be measured without violating privacy?

Do not add a channel because it is fashionable. Add it because it improves reach, trust, action or learning for a defined cohort.

## 5. The public funnel

| Stage | Person's question | Communication job | Strong AQOON formats | Primary measurement |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **TOF - Discover** | "I did not know this existed" | make the right person recognise a real problem, right or opportunity | problem-led direct-to-camera video, timely opening, myth, relatable story, short field observation | qualified reach, new viewers, shares and relevant profile/page visits |
| **MOF - Understand and trust** | "Does this concern me, and can I trust this?" | explain one important fact, remove the main misunderstanding and show the route | fact/value sequence, Q&A, comparison, verified result story, founder/field context, longer guide, trusted connector explanation | saves, return viewers, link actions, identifiable contacts and completed intakes |
| **BOF - Ask and act** | "How do I actually do this?" | make the next step safe, specific and easy | application or registration demonstration, checklist, documents/timeline, private call, interview and personalised follow-up | interview, verified match, assisted action and handoff |
| **Outcome and learning** | "Did it work and continue?" | verify the result, preserve the relationship and learn without exposing the person | outcome call, persistence check, anonymised story with the right permission, updated FAQ or route content | verified outcome, persistence, repeat contact and new PII-free learning |

The operating constraint determines the next content priority. If AQOON has more contacts than it can call or interview, more TOF reach is not the priority. Fix response capacity and use MOF/BOF material to help existing families progress. If qualified people are not entering, improve the entry problem, channel, hook and CTA. If applications stall, improve the route and operational follow-up rather than blaming content.

### B2B search and content funnel

The buyer funnel uses the same TOF/MOF/BOF logic with a different promise and destination:

| Stage | Buyer question | Content job | Best owned destination | Proof |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| TOF | "Why are relevant Somali-speaking people not finding, trusting or completing our service?" | name the missed-demand or broken-journey problem in buyer language | a focused B2B insight page on `aqoon.live` | qualified search visibility and engaged buyer visits |
| MOF | "Can AQOON diagnose and operate this better?" | show method, measured stages, barriers and anonymised evidence | `havainnot/`, `menetelma/` or `tapaus/` | evidence-page depth, return visits and qualified conversations |
| BOF | "What exactly would we buy and how would it work?" | define scope, responsibilities, measurement, privacy and next decision | `paketit/` or a scoped contact/booking route | qualified meeting, proposal and decision |

B2B SEO topics include Somali integration and service reach only when AQOON can connect the query to a real buyer problem and a truthful capability. They must not stereotype Somalis, imply that a community is inventory or expose family data.

## 6. Content pillars

Every family-facing asset should belong to at least one pillar:

1. **Opportunity now:** a real opening, programme, place, deadline or useful route.
2. **"I did not know":** an option, right or service that is missing from people's consideration.
3. **Misunderstanding removal:** price, eligibility boundary, authority role, consequence, application or timing confusion.
4. **Step by step:** the next form, document, registration, call or application stage.
5. **One real question:** a direct answer to a question heard in calls, interviews, DMs, search or fieldwork.
6. **Verified journey/result:** an anonymised, truthful chain from problem to action or outcome. State what remains unknown.
7. **Founder and community trust:** why AQOON exists, how Abducadir checks information and what has been learned from helping people. This is useful context, not unrelated lifestyle content.

## 7. Format roles

- **Direct-to-camera / handheld explanation:** Abducadir answers one real question in his own voice.
- **Story-led video:** recognisable situation -> missing information -> useful turning point -> next action.
- **Fact explainer:** one decision-changing fact, its source boundary and what the viewer should do.
- **Value sequence:** two to four connected parts that move from discovery to understanding to action.
- **Tutorial or live demonstration:** official page, registration, application or document step shown carefully.
- **Ask AQOON:** one specific submitted question answered thoroughly.
- **Result sequence:** only a verified, anonymised outcome with the stages and limitations named.
- **Founder/field context:** demonstrates method, care and credibility without exposing a family.
- **Community share pack:** short message, approved visual/video, exact tagged link, disclosure text and a contact instruction a trusted person or järjestö can pass on unchanged.
- **Evergreen guide:** durable search page that answers the question and offers the same clear help route.

Generic lifestyle, unrelated viral commentary and trends with no connection to a real AQOON need are not strategy.

## 8. Where ideas come from

The content queue is fed by evidence, not brainstorming alone. Prioritise:

1. repeated family questions and blockers in first interviews, calls and DMs;
2. verified routes, openings and deadlines from current official/provider sources;
3. funnel drop-off and follow-up friction in the Tracker;
4. real partner capacity and cohort needs;
5. successful and unsuccessful past topics by downstream movement, not views alone;
6. search queries and guidance-page entry behaviour;
7. questions received through trusted mothers, known people, järjestöt and fieldwork;
8. PII-free outcome learning.

The current reviewed TikTok performance input is `../../evidence-and-research/references/tiktok-analytics-2026-09-01.md`. It is a source for content-performance hypotheses, not proof that an older service claim is still current and not a replacement for CRM or outcome evidence.

Before production, every topic receives:

- primary audience and municipality/cohort;
- entry problem or opportunity;
- funnel stage;
- source/evidence and verification date;
- one desired next action;
- channel and format;
- CTA and landing/contact route;
- source/campaign tracking values;
- owner and response capacity;
- privacy, consent, partner and commercial-disclosure requirements.

If the fact is high-volatility or live, verify it again before publication. A previously successful video is not a current source.

## 9. The AQOON script structure

Use five parts. Short videos may compress them, but they must keep the same logic.

1. **Relevant hook:** name the person, situation, problem or opportunity immediately. No long logo or generic introduction.
2. **Useful answer:** give the one fact or explanation that rewards attention.
3. **Remove the barrier:** answer the main misunderstanding, fear or reason people stop.
4. **Safe route:** explain the next step and what still must be confirmed by the authority/provider.
5. **One CTA:** choose one action only.

This adapts Hook-Story-Offer and Hook-Retain-Reward for AQOON. The final element is not pressure to buy. It is a safe, useful next step.

### Length patterns

**15-second reminder**

`specific hook -> one fact/deadline -> one CTA`

**30-45-second explainer**

`specific hook -> why it matters -> one useful fact -> main misunderstanding -> next step -> CTA`

**60-90-second guide or demonstration**

`specific hook -> situation -> ordered steps -> documents/timing -> boundary -> CTA`

**Three-part value sequence**

1. discovery/problem;
2. understanding/trust;
3. action/how AQOON helps.

### Production rules

- Family-facing video is Somali-first and sounds like a real person, not translated institutional copy.
- Abducadir uses his own voice. Do not use AI characters, AI avatars or a replacement AI voice.
- Use native vertical phone video when appropriate, with clear CapCut on-screen text and subtitles.
- Keep Finnish institution and system terms such as `Kela`, `päiväkoti`, `palveluseteli` and `Työmarkkinatori` in Finnish when that is what the family must recognise.
- One video, one main idea, one CTA.
- Human-review every Somali version before publication.
- Do not show family PII, private messages, forms, documents or a recognisable story without the necessary permission and minimisation.
- If a creator collaboration is paid, gifted or otherwise commercial, use the current KKV-compliant disclosure. Written disclosure must be immediately clear; spoken disclosure is added when written marking is not accessible enough.

## 10. CTA and landing rules

The CTA promises the next useful action, not AQOON in the abstract.

- **Default family CTA:** `aqoon.live/caawi` or the relevant `/caawi/<topic>` page followed by the `/caawi` intake, where the person gives name/phone first and receives a callback.
- **DM, WhatsApp or phone CTA:** use only when that private route is intentionally selected, staffed and attributed. Existing evidence indicates many families prefer a private phone route over a public group.
- **Community CTA:** the family contacts AQOON directly. A connector does not send family identities unless the person has given the correct separate permission.
- **Buyer CTA:** one scoped conversation about the missing outcome or broken journey.

Do not place four competing options in one asset. If urgency requires direct contact, a TOF asset may use the `/caawi` CTA immediately.

Before publication, test the exact landing page or contact route on mobile. A strong video with a broken form is a failed campaign.

## 11. Trusted connector, influencer and järjestö network

Build this as a permission-based community distribution network, not a purchased influencer list.

### Connector roles

- **Distributor:** shares an approved message or link with a relevant audience.
- **Explainer/co-creator:** joins Abducadir to answer a real community question.
- **Host:** järjestö or trusted actor creates a place for AQOON to explain and receive private contacts.
- **Referrer:** tells an individual that AQOON can help and lets that person contact AQOON directly.

### Network record

For each connector or organisation, record only legitimate business relationship information:

- name and actor type;
- municipality/community and languages;
- why this actor is relevant and trusted;
- agreed role and topics;
- contact owner and permission/status;
- paid, gifted, reciprocal or unpaid relationship;
- disclosure requirement;
- approved asset and tagged source/link;
- campaign/cohort;
- dates, results and next action.

Do not store a connector's private audience or family list. Do not ask a järjestö or mother to reveal who needs help.

### Distribution workflow

1. Define the verified problem/opportunity, cohort, timing and follow-up capacity.
2. Select connectors who genuinely match that cohort.
3. Agree the role, payment/benefit, wording, disclosure and boundaries.
4. Give a small share pack: one approved message, asset, tagged link/contact route and FAQ.
5. The connector shares or hosts. Interested people contact AQOON voluntarily.
6. AQOON records the source, responds privately and runs the normal interview/outcome journey.
7. Report aggregate progression and barriers back to the connector/partner. Do not expose family cases.

Never mass-spam groups, scrape member lists or treat community trust as transferable inventory.

## 12. Campaign workflow

Every campaign follows the same sequence, whether it starts with TikTok, the Yellow Card domain, a known mother, a järjestö, fieldwork or a partner.

1. **Outcome:** define the real action/outcome and what does not count.
2. **Cohort:** define who, where, when and why now.
3. **Truth and capacity:** verify the route, criteria, availability, deadline and who will answer.
4. **Bottleneck:** decide whether the current problem is reach, understanding, contact, interview, action, handoff or persistence.
5. **Channel mix:** select the trusted owned, social, community, field and partner paths needed for that bottleneck.
6. **Message:** one entry problem, useful reward and CTA.
7. **Assets:** script/video/page/share pack plus source tags.
8. **Review:** facts, Somali, privacy, consent, commercial disclosure and mobile handoff.
9. **Launch and operate:** publish/distribute, respond, interview and move the cohort.
10. **Review and learn:** compare stages, speed, barriers, outcomes and persistence; create PII-free improvements.

Do not launch demand without a named follow-up owner and realistic response capacity.

## 13. Measurement and attribution

The current Caawi runtime stores `referrer_host`, `utm_source`, `utm_medium` and `utm_campaign`. Use them consistently:

- `utm_source`: origin such as `tiktok`, `caawi_seo`, `yellow_card_domain`, `whatsapp`, `phone_referral`, `field`, `jarjesto`, `trusted_connector` or `partner`;
- `utm_medium`: delivery type such as `organic`, `social`, `creator`, `community`, `referral`, `field`, `paid` or `partner`;
- `utm_campaign`: a governed, non-PII campaign or asset ID that can be mapped to the internal campaign record;
- `referrer_host`: captured automatically where available.

The schema does not currently have first-class `source_actor`, `source_asset`, `campaign_id` or `entry_problem` fields. Keep those in the governed campaign record and map them through `utm_campaign` until an explicit runtime migration is implemented. Never place a person's name, phone number or case detail in a URL or UTM value. Keep first known source and final contact route conceptually separate; do not infer missing attribution.

The core scorecard is:

1. relevant reach and engagement;
2. identifiable contacts;
3. completed intakes;
4. first interviews;
5. verified matches;
6. assisted actions;
7. handoffs;
8. verified outcomes;
9. persistence;
10. time between stages;
11. confirmed additional needs per acquired family;
12. repeat contact and referral source.

Views, saves and followers diagnose distribution. They are not family outcomes. Do not divide blended organic and paid reach into a paid cost-per-lead claim. Use matched campaign spend and attributable conversions from the same cohort.

## 14. Pre-publication gate

Do not publish or distribute until all critical items are true:

- [ ] one primary audience and one concrete problem/opportunity;
- [ ] current source and verification date where the claim can change;
- [ ] no unsupported eligibility or outcome promise;
- [ ] one useful reward, not empty curiosity;
- [ ] one next action and a working mobile route;
- [ ] campaign/source tracking decided;
- [ ] named owner and capacity to respond;
- [ ] privacy, consent and story-use boundaries checked;
- [ ] paid partner/creator relationship disclosed where required;
- [ ] Somali reviewed by a human;
- [ ] buyer and family purposes are not mixed.

## 17. What AQOON does not do

- generic "we help with everything" acquisition messages;
- unrelated viral or lifestyle content for reach alone;
- AI characters or replacement voices in family-facing content;
- fear, shame, fake urgency or fabricated scarcity;
- hidden paid recommendations or disguised influencer marketing;
- eligibility promises or stale public-service instructions;
- family stories, screens or messages used as content without the correct permission;
- group spam, scraped member lists or purchased community access;
- several CTAs competing in one asset;
- reporting views, leads, applications and outcomes as the same result;
- creating more demand than AQOON or the partner can responsibly operate.
