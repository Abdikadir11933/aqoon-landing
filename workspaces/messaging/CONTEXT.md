# Messaging Context

Inputs: audience, intended action, canonical evidence IDs, brand voice and channel constraints.

Rules: factual claims must trace to evidence/source IDs; do not turn hypotheses, one-provider observations or stale statistics into universal claims. Private buyer/sales material stays outside public GitHub.

## Current acquisition architecture

AQOON should not lead with a broad list of everything it can help with. The strongest current pattern is:

**one concrete problem/opportunity → useful Somali-language explanation → one clear CTA → `/caawi` → phone captured → first interview → verified match/action → follow-up**

The entry problem is the acquisition door, not the permanent definition of the family relationship.

Examples of entry doors:
- a free hobby or registration opening;
- a daycare choice or application problem;
- a work/training opportunity;
- a school or language-support issue;
- a current programme with clear eligibility.

The CTA should normally promise the next useful action, not advertise AQOON abstractly: fill the short form, AQOON gets in touch, and the person receives help with the specific next step.

## Message hierarchy

Family-facing:
1. concrete relevance: "this may apply to you / your child";
2. one useful fact that changes a decision;
3. remove the biggest misunderstanding or friction;
4. one simple next step;
5. human follow-up when needed.

Buyer-facing:
1. do not sell views alone;
2. show the measurable chain from distribution to identifiable contact to interview to action/outcome;
3. explain that AQOON combines trusted distribution, navigation and outcome measurement;
4. distinguish proven evidence from the next hypothesis being tested.

## Claim language

Safe now:
- AQOON has demonstrated that useful Somali-language content can generate identifiable help requests.
- The Pilke work has already demonstrated at least one end-to-end daycare path from contact through applications to a real start.
- The new intake/CRM makes the path easier to measure and makes some incomplete requests recoverable after phone capture.
- Current demand entering the funnel spans several need categories.

Still a hypothesis:
- one acquired family will reliably generate multiple additional service-navigation needs;
- current early conversion rates will hold at scale;
- current blended acquisition economics equal paid-ad CPA.

Never turn an early cohort into a permanent benchmark.
