# Strategy context

Use for an honest assessment of AQOON's position, opportunities, offer design, recurring revenue, buyer acquisition, delivery capacity or potential pivots. This workspace contains a public method, not the private business plan.

## Inputs

Read `docs/architecture/business-operating-model.md`, the evidence index at `workspaces/evidence-and-research/references/aqoon-evidence-index.md` and only the dated evidence needed. Obtain current financial, pipeline, delivery and time-capacity facts from authorized private sources when the decision depends on them. Missing access remains a limitation; prior conversation does not prove current system access.

## Process

1. Separate observed demand, founder-reported facts, buyer willingness to pay, verified outcomes, economics and hypotheses. Attention, contacts, proposals, signed work, invoicing and cash received are different stages.
2. Diagnose the actual constraint from reliable evidence and founder-confirmed commitments. Do not infer an overdue backlog or missed interviews from unmaintained CRM dates.
3. Compare a small set of plausible options against buyer urgency, budget owner, sales cycle, repeatability, delivery load, contribution margin, cash timing and founder capacity. Do not invent financial precision where costs or dates are unresolved.
4. Test whether trusted language, explanation, distribution and navigation can support adjacent markets, commercial partnerships or an owned product. The current approved offer is a baseline for comparison, not a rule forbidding a pivot. Keep proposed changes separate from public promises.
5. Recommend what to continue, cut, test or postpone. Each proposed experiment needs a time/cost bound, observable success or failure signal and a decision it will inform.

## Outputs

A private decision brief: current position, strongest evidence, material uncertainties, ranked options, recommended focus and a realistic execution plan. Publicly reusable learning can be sanitized into evidence references after review. Update approved website messaging only when that change is part of the authorized task.

Use `docs/briefs/task-handoff-template.md` for continuity. For website criticism, use `.claude/skills/website-review/SKILL.md`; do not replace the requested website audit with a general strategy exercise.
