# AQOON demand and content workflow

Read `workspaces/messaging/CONTEXT.md`, then select the relevant sections of `workspaces/messaging/references/aqoon-demand-generation-and-content-os.md`: sections 1–3 and 10 for audience/claim boundaries; 6–9 and 15–16 for ideas/scripts; 4–5 and 11–13 for campaigns, connectors or performance. Read section 14 before a publication-ready handoff. Read `BRAND.md` for visual decisions. Use the evidence index for current observations; do not reload every historical snapshot.

For a claim about a current service, deadline, criterion, fee, capacity or public rule, also read `workspaces/evidence-and-research/CONTEXT.md` and verify the current official/provider source. Do not use a past video or old campaign as current proof.

## Choose the mode

- **Idea or content plan:** build the standard content brief before proposing formats.
- **Video or script:** complete the content brief, then use Hook -> Help -> Barrier -> Route -> one CTA.
- **Campaign:** define outcome, cohort, verified truth/capacity, bottleneck, channel mix, handoff owner and measurement before producing assets.
- **Trusted connector, creator or järjestö distribution:** define the actor's role, permission, compensation/benefit, disclosure, share pack, tagged route and privacy-safe reporting.
- **Performance review:** separate reach, identifiable contact, intake, interview, match, action, handoff, outcome and persistence. Assess demand, audience, problems, geography and buyer implications. Establish constraints from reliable evidence or explicit founder confirmation; unmaintained follow-up dates and statuses cannot establish a backlog or missed interviews.

## Required decisions

Every deliverable must make clear:

- primary audience: family/adult, buyer/partner or community connector;
- canonical surface: `aqoon.live/caawi` for families or the `aqoon.live` B2B site for buyers;
- concrete entry problem/opportunity and funnel stage;
- evidence/source and verification date when the claim can change;
- one useful reward and one next action;
- channel, format and source tracking;
- who can answer and operate the demand created;
- privacy, consent, story-use and commercial-disclosure boundaries;
- success measure beyond views.

The founder is the named public representative, but appearing on camera is optional. Faceless explainers are an established format. Prioritize useful topics, understandable Somali, familiar delivery, relevant cultural context and trusted contact. Do not assume religion or preferences from ethnicity. Follow the canonical production rules for any proposed synthetic media; do not invent how historical audio was produced. Keep Somali human-reviewed and avoid hidden partner steering, competing CTAs or unsupported promises.

Use `/caawi` as the default family handoff unless another private contact route is deliberately selected, staffed and attributed. Family SEO and Somali help pages live under `/caawi/<topic>`. Buyer SEO lives on the B2B root and established B2B pages and targets organisation problems such as Somali integration, trusted reach, service navigation, conversion, onboarding and persistence. Never mix the two search intents or CTAs.

TikTok, the Yellow Card domain, Caawi SEO, B2B SEO, DMs, WhatsApp, phone, fieldwork, trusted mothers/known people, järjestöt, community actors, referrals, paid promotion and buyer outreach are channels inside one engine, not separate businesses. `/so/*` is redirect-only and must not be used in new assets.

For family attribution, use only the runtime fields that exist: `referrer_host`, `utm_source`, `utm_medium` and `utm_campaign`. Use a non-PII `utm_campaign` ID to map an asset or connector in the governed campaign record; do not claim `source_asset` or `source_actor` are live schema fields.

Do not publish, contact external people, alter live routing or start paid promotion unless the user explicitly authorises that external action. Producing or updating the approved repository strategy does not itself authorise distribution.

Before handing off a production-ready asset, run the canonical pre-publication gate. If a critical item is unknown, mark it explicitly rather than inventing it.
