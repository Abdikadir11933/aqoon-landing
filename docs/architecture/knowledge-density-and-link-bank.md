# Knowledge Density and Link-bank Decision

## Decision

Yes: AQOON needs a link bank, but it must be a source registry, not a giant flat bookmark dump.

Dense records should contain only routing-critical information: stable ID, authority, scope, current status/effective date, volatility, last verification, exact source IDs, matching fields and authority-decision boundary. Explanations belong below frontmatter only when they help a human/agent interpret the record.

## Missing coverage to add over time

The current verified seed covers the highest-risk national routes but is not an exhaustive catalogue of every Finnish authority/provider. Priority expansion:
1. Kela benefit-by-benefit records and calculator mapping.
2. city-specific early-childhood routes for Helsinki, Vantaa, Espoo, Tampere.
3. city/employment-area jobseeker, integration and entrepreneurship routes.
4. school support, S2, preparatory education and hobby pathways by city.
5. adult Finnish/integration/qualification routes and Studyinfo providers.
6. wellbeing-services-county health/social routes.
7. DVV, tax, permits, housing, debt/consumer advice and family-law navigation.
8. live programme/job research handled at case time, never cached as timeless truth.

The source registry is intentionally expandable without changing the production route structure.
