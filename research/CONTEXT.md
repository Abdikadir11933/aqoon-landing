# Research context

`research/` contains dated evidence-gathering work. Research is not production truth until reconciled into `knowledge/`.

## Research workflow

1. Define the user question and scope.
2. Inventory existing AQOON claims that could be affected.
3. Find the responsible authority/provider.
4. Open the live official source.
5. Record exactly what the source supports, plus checked date and scope.
6. Flag conflicts, uncertainty and stale AQOON content.
7. Reconcile verified facts into canonical `knowledge/` records.
8. Only then update `/so`, `/caawi`, tracker matching logic or operator material.

## Finland-wide research target

The long-term target is a comprehensive navigational and explanatory database of relevant Finnish official services, programmes, rights/rules, calculators, forms, deadlines and contact routes for AQOON users.

Research should include both national authorities and city/local implementation. Never generalize a city rule to Finland as a whole.

## Deep-research outputs

Deep research reports belong here conceptually, but only durable verified records should be promoted to `knowledge/`.
