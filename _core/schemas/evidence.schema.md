# Evidence Record Schema

```yaml
---
id: evidence.<domain>.<slug>
record_type: evidence
claim:
evidence_type: aqoon-primary | official | provider | research
source_ids: []
observed_at:
method:
sample:
limitations:
status: verified | verification_pending | retired
allowed_uses: []
prohibited_inferences: []
---
```

Evidence supports only the claim at its documented scope. Do not silently generalise pilot/provider observations into national facts.
