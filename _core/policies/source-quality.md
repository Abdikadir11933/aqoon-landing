# Source Quality Policy

## A. AQOON primary evidence
Use for AQOON-owned observations: pilots, sanitized analytics, aggregated CRM metrics, documented interviews. Preserve date, method, sample and limitations. Internet verification is not a substitute for primary evidence.

## B. Official primary
Use Finlex, Kela, Migri, OPH, Työmarkkinatori, municipalities, wellbeing services counties and other competent authorities for legal rules, eligibility, public processes and authority responsibilities.

## C. Provider primary
Use the specific provider/employer/school/programme page for current provider-specific availability, requirements, schedules, prices and deadlines.

## D. Research / secondary
Use for context and business evidence. Do not make it the sole basis for a person's legal entitlement or official route when a primary authority source exists.

## E. Discovery only
Search results, aggregators and old internal notes may identify what to investigate. They are not sufficient evidence for a final claim.

## Claim rule
A source must support the exact claim at the same scope. A true source does not justify a broader claim than it actually states.

## Missing proof
If the primary source cannot be found, mark the claim `verification_pending` rather than upgrading a weaker source by convenience.
