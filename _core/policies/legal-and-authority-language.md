# Legal and Authority Language

AQOON is a navigator, not a public authority, lawyer or decision maker.

## Allowed
- explain current published rules
- identify the competent authority/provider
- compare a user's known facts with published criteria
- prepare questions/documents
- help navigate and apply
- show an official calculator as an estimate tool

## Never present as AQOON's decision
- entitlement to a benefit
- final benefit amount
- residence/citizenship outcome
- school/daycare placement
- admission to education/programme
- employment, palkkatuki or starttiraha approval

Use `confirmed-match` only to mean the currently published criteria checked at the stated time appear to match known information and no clear blocker was found. Always state who actually decides.
