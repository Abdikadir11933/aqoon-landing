# Route Safety QA

Every recommendation must answer:
- what need is being solved?
- which facts matter?
- who is the competent authority/provider?
- what is the current source and rule?
- what is missing or conflicting?
- what is the match status?
- what is the exact next action?
- who actually decides?

No benefit, permit, job, grant, admission, school/daycare placement or programme outcome may be promised by AQOON.
